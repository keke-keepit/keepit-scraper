As the world’s largest independent Salesforce community and media site, Salesforce Ben lives
and breathes Salesforce. Headquartered in the UK, the company provides news, education,
and insights to Salesforce professionals around the world. With team members working across
multiple SaaS platforms, ensuring business continuity is essential to keeping content and
community operations running smoothly.

needed a proper backup and recovery solution.”
