How to successfully adopt AI capabilities
Successful AI adoption follows a simple framework: Start with the outcome by defining the decision or
process to improve, along with clear success metrics and acceptable risk; establish controls through
policies and technical guardrails such as data boundaries, model scope, agent conditions, auditability, and
rollback paths; and finally, drive enablement by providing approved tools, patterns, and sandboxes that
meet compliance and sovereignty needs, allowing teams to innovate safely and at speed.
