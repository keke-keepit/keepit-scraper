customer. Therefore, no human operators outside of the customer’s own organization are involved in accessing,
Automation

However, there are no recurring or routine tasks that require human operators to access customer data. Furthermore,
Restricted access
there are technical measures in place to ensure that no such access is required and that it is not easily or accidentally
invoked. Additionally, there are organizational measures in place to educate and train staff on information security
practices and policies.

staff in an appropriate region, considering data sovereignty requirements, support availability, and time constraints.
