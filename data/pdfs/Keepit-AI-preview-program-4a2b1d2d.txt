Keepit AI
preview program
About the program
AI offers real opportunities to create customer value, but with so many possibilities, it’s difficult to
know which ones matter most. That’s why we’re turning to you. We want to work with our customers
to explore and identify AI capabilities that solve genuine challenges in managing and protecting data.
The Keepit MCP Integration is our first initiative in this direction. We’re inviting you to test it and share
your feedback as we continue developing AI features for the platform.
What’s MCP?
Model Context Protocol is an open standard that connects AI applications directly to your business
systems.
What this enables today
Our initial surface is read-only by design. It focuses on low-risk, high-value queries admins already
ask, such as:
• Reviewing connector posture and highlighting unhealthy states with likely root causes to check
(for example, misconfiguration).
• Inspecting jobs and snapshot ranges to explain recent failures.
• Pulling scoped audit logs and assembling summaries suitable for governance stakeholders.
