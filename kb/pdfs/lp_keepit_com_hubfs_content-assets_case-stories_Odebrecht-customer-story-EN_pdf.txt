A real incident revealed the gap
Odebrecht had solid backup coverage for its on-
premises systems, but Microsoft 365, which had 
become the primary environment for collaboration and 
productivity across the group, was a different story. 
The team was relying entirely on Microsoft's standard 
retention, with no independent backup and no 
contractual guarantee of recovery./ 

The gap became undeniable during a proof of 
concept. An employee accidentally deleted a shared 
OneDrive folder containing approximately 32,000 
documents from an active construction project. Keepit 
restored everything, complete with a full integrity 
report, during the evaluation itself. That incident moved 
the decision from consideration to commitment.

A list of demanding requirements
Odebrecht partnered with Adapta to select and 
deploy a backup and recovery solution that could 
meet its evolving needs./ 

Odebrecht evaluated four vendors against 
detailed technical, contractual, and operational 
requirements. The priorities were full coverage of 
Microsoft 365 SaaS applications, predictable costs 
with unlimited storage, no egress fees, and no 
contractual lock-in that would complicate a future 
exit. Keepit was the only vendor to meet all the 
criteria, making it the clear choice on every 
dimension./ 

Fast recovery  streamlined compliance/
Since going into full production, Odebrecht has 
completed more than 20 recovery processes. The 
compliance team now runs investigations 
independently, averaging around six cases per
month without routing requests through IT. Keepit 
was also demonstrated live during an external ISO 
37001 audit and approved./

Today, Odebrecht uses Keepit to protect 
approximately 794 TB of Microsoft 365 data, 
alongside Microsoft Entra ID, Power Platform, Azure 
DevOps, and DocuSign, all stored in Keepit's own 
independent cloud./ 

A platform ready for the AI era/(
As Odebrecht expands its use of AI agents across 
its SaaS environment, those agents operate on the 
same platforms where critical data lives, at a speed 
and scale that makes real-time human oversight 
impossible. Having an immutable, independent 
copy of that data acts as an anchor of reality. It 
gives the team confidence to move fast, knowing 
the record of what's true can't be altered by any 
automated process./

About Odebrecht
With more than eight decades of history, Odebrecht Engineering & Construction is the heavy construction 
company of the Novonor Group, providing services in the infrastructure and construction, industrial 
assembly, and maintenance segments. The company has completed more than 3,000 projects in 37 
countries around the world, including hydroelectric, thermal, and nuclear power plants, metro systems, 
railways, bridges, roads, airports, and refineries. Headquartered in Brazil, it primarily operates across three
