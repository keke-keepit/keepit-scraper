It Simply Works!
According to SEP Administration Director Susanne 
Moosreiner, Keepit provides a complete cloud-based 
solution that is easy to set up, easy to use, and ensures 
GDPR compliance. While other vendors require additional 
storage and retention fees, Keepit allows SEP to offer an 
all-inclusive seat pricing to their clients. 
GDPR Compliance & Data Sovereignty 
Compliance and data protection regulations are of the 
utmost importance for SEP and their clients. As a German 
and European vendor, SEP is required to comply to GDPR 
regulations and ensure that all client data is stored within 
the EU. As data remains within Europe/Germany, SEP can 
guarantee full control, ensuring data sovereignty. 
It is key for us and our clients that 
Keepit has data centers in Europe and 
is compliant with GDPR regulations.
Susanne Moosreiner
