Cognita and Keepit:  
Scaling operations during 
international expansion  
Customer case:
Cognita

A platform that covers multiple workloads 
seamlessly 
As a leading global schools group, Cognita and 
its IT team take cybersecurity seriously. As they 
were embarking upon a standardization of their 
group processes, including re-architecting their 
systems, backing up Microsoft 365, Microsoft 
Entra ID and Google Workspace data was a top 
priority. Keepit came recommended to Cognita 
by Phoenix Software, a longstanding partner 
focused on modernizing workplaces through 
cutting-edge technologies. 
The Keepit platform gives full visibility and control 
over Cognita’s systems, providing one scalable 
backup platform that covers multiple SaaS 
workloads. This helps Cognita to be confident 
that all their critical data is backed up and easy to 
monitor. “We’ve now got complete confidence 
and visibility that all of our Microsoft 365 and 
Google Workspace user data is being backed up 
on a single platform,” says James Mallett, Head of 
Global Infrastructure at Cognita. He continues; 
“Since Microsoft doesn’t guarantee our data, 
I need a solution like Keepit. Data retention is 
absolutely key for us, and ultimately the data is my 
responsibility.”
Confidence and support in achieving data 
residency 
With increasing regulations putting a spotlight 
on data sovereignty, Cognita is ensuring their 
ability to respond to any and all requirements 
coming their way. “Data residency is definitely a 
conversation we’re following. We are continuously 
engaging with experts in our regions to ensure 
that we are doing the right things.  A solution like 
Keepit can help us stay ahead of the curve by 
providing data centers in multiple locations,” says 
James Mallett.
With seven data center regions, across the 
Americas, Europe, UK, and Asia-Pacific, Keepit 
can help Cognita store data in varying locations 
across the world, taking into consideration local 
regulations and requirements.
“My job is to ensure business continuity, 
compliance and resilience. I wouldn’t be 
fulfilling my responsibilities if I didn’t rely on 
a third-party backup provider like Keepit.” 
 - James Mallett, Head of Global Infrastructure,  Cognita
Customer case:
Cognita
Growing at a rapid pace,  global schools group Cognita required a compliant and reliable backup 
and recovery platform to secure their vast amounts of sensitive student data in Microsoft 365 
and Google Workspace. During the process of enhancing their group systems, Keepit has 
proven to be a crucial partner by supporting the organization to scale and meet ever-increasing 
cybersecurity standards, provide cost-effective backup and prepare the schools to tackle an 
evolving regulatory landscape.
“A solution like Keepit can help us stay ahead 
of the curve by providing data centers in 
multiple locations.”
- James Mallett, Head of Global Infrastructure, Cognita

About Cognita:
Launched in 2004, Cognita is an 
extraordinary community of schools that 
share one common purpose: empowering 
students to thrive in a rapidly evolving 
world. With over 100 schools in Europe 
and the US, the Middle East, Latin America, 
and Asia, Cognita employs over 21,000 
teaching and support staff in the care 
and education of more than 95,000 
students. Together, Cognita schools 
provide a uniquely global education that 
goes beyond grades to develop all-round 
academic excellence – equipping young 
people with the agency, adaptability and 
positive attitudes that prepares them to 
grow, thrive and find their success in a 
rapidly evolving world.
About Phoenix Software:
Phoenix provides IT solutions and managed 
services to meet the unique objectives 
of each organization they work with. 
Their approach focuses on modernizing 
workplaces through the utilization of 
cutting-edge technologies such as cloud 
and hybrid infrastructure, AI, cyber security, 
collaboration, and productivity solutions.
A platform that is easy to use – and budget 
“The benefit with Keepit is that we can easily replace the 
users when we need to – and because it’s a flat fee per user, 
we have the ability to control our budgets,” explains James 
Mallett. 
With unlimited data retention and storage, Cognita has 
one less thing to worry about when dealing with student 
data.  Other Keepit features such as the data monitoring 
dashboard facilitate full visibility and control over data, and 
who is accessing it. 
Ease of use also ensures that limited IT resources are used 
appropriately.  “Like all other IT teams, we don’t have enough 
hours in the day to do all the things we’d like, so an easy, 
smooth interface is a huge advantage to Cognita,” explains 
James Mallett. 
 
Easy integration and future-proof backup  
Keepit is easily integrated with existing, automated 
processes, giving IT and HR the confidence that employee 
data is backed up and retained properly, all throughout the 
user lifecycle. Adding, moving and removing employees 
is seamlessly synchronized with Microsoft 365 and Keepit 
licensing to ensure accuracy and business continuity.
“I’ve used different backup platforms for Microsoft 365. I 
found that Keepit is a lot more responsive and a lot quicker,” 
says James Mallett when describing the Keepit platform. 
“We are really looking forward to rolling out Keepit backup 
and recovery for other important applications.”
The Keepit platform is poised to facilitate even more 
workloads, ensuring companies like Cognita can secure 
more applications through a one-stop shop solution.
“With Keepit, I can confidently attend any SLT 
meeting and can assure them that every user data 
is backed up.”
- James Mallett, Head of Global Infrastructure, Cognita
