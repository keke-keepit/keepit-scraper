Behind every successful IT company is a great 
product. But even the best product stands and 
falls with the quality of its sales and customer 
service.  
 
Only those who know the needs and problems 
of their customers can provide them with the 
best advice and offer the best solutions to 
address their individual challenges. 
 
In the realm of sales, the customer’s needs 
come first. The more aligned a company’s sales 
strategy is with its customers’ needs, the better 
the sales results are likely to be.  
 
For this reason, large IT companies and IT 
service specialists must maintain a balanced 
product and service portfolio that includes the 
right solution for every customer request. 
 
There are, however, fundamental requirements, 
like business continuity management, that 
all companies have to address, since reliable 
backup is a key element in this area.

Partner case: Serviceware
2
How Keepit and Serviceware 
identified their synergies
 It was a mutual acquaintance - in the form of a 
Keepit OEM technology partner - that brought 
Keepit and Serviceware together in 2021. 
 
After initial discussions, it quickly became clear 
to both sides that a great deal of potential 
could be drawn from their shared strengths.  
 
When Keepit considered Serviceware as a 
potential partner, it was the large customer 
base - over 1,400 customers in the DACH 
region alone - as well as the proven expertise 
in business continuity management that 
convinced Keepit. As the market leader in 
enterprise service management with over 
500 employees at 14 international locations, 
Serviceware met all the criteria for an optimal 
partnership. 
 
The concentrated manpower in combination 
with a high degree of technical understanding 
made the company particularly attractive 
to Keepit. Additionally, the Danish backup 
specialist pursues a partner-first channel 
strategy and therefore places great value on 
close and trusting partnerships.

Business continuity management 
and cloud migration
Many companies only realize the enormous 
importance of BCM when they fall victim to a 
cyberattack. In the worst-case scenario, they 
are then left without access to their critical IT 
systems and structures from one moment to 
the next. 
 
In such a precarious situation, having a 
functioning plan up your sleeve for continuing 
or restoring business operations is worth its 
weight in gold – especially since the NIS2 
directive has become mandatory for many 
companies based in the EU.  
 
Such an emergency plan not only helps to take 
the right steps promptly in this - often chaotic 
– moment; in fact, in some cases of hardship, 
it even decides on the further existence of the 
company. 
 
Unfortunately, customers still underestimate 
the risk of losing data within a SaaS application 
and neglect their responsibility as owners of 
the data. 
 
“Many companies are not aware that the 
responsibility for their data in the cloud lies with 
themselves. Those responsible for security 
rely completely on the cloud provider - a fatal 
error,” says Michael Heuer, VP Central Europe 
(DACH) at Keepit, and adds: “After a migration 
of company data to the cloud, you often have 
even less control than before.”

The right chemistry 
Since the start of the partnership, both 
companies have been in close contact with each 
other and have been developing individualized 
solutions for their diverse client portfolio. 
The dedicated product managers are in regular 
contact with each other and thus ensure that 
the required know-how is available to all parties 
involved and that customers can be optimally 
advised. In joint webinars, relevant product 
innovations are communicated, and specialist 
personnel are trained in detail regarding the 
installation, use and maintenance of Keepit’s 
backup systems. Personal contact is guaranteed 
by the regular presence of Keepit employees at 
Serviceware’s headquarters.

Shaping the 
future together
The two specialists have taken the opportunity 
of being a part of this trusting cooperation 
to look to the future together. One particular 
project of note involves combining the ITSM 
product interfaces from the Serviceware 
portfolio with Keepit’s backup solution. This 
should enable customers to hand over their 
data recovery requirements even more easily 
via the user helpdesk service portal. 
Joint, industry-specific campaigns and 
approaches are also planned to tap into new 
market segments.  
With these words, Keepit’s Michael Heuer sums 
up the fruitful cooperation and looks forward to 
a promising future with Serviceware.
