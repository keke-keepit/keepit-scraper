With 89 years of history and brands such as Dona Benta, Sol, Petybon, Brandini, and Boa 
Sorte on shelves across Brazil, J.Macêdo is one of the country's largest food companies. Its 
users work entirely through Microsoft 365, relying on SharePoint, Outlook, and Microsoft 
Entra ID as the backbone of daily collaboration. When the IT team, led by Luciano Bortoluzzi, 
head of information technology, evaluated how to protect that environment, three priorities 
shaped the search: ease of use, reliable recovery, and the ability to support the company's 
compliance and audit programs without adding complexity.

Fast recovery, flexible options
For J.Macêdo, data protection isn't just about 
having a copy of data somewhere. It's about being 
able to get that data back fast, in the right format, 
when the business needs it.

Recovery options make all the difference. 
Bortoluzzi's team can restore data directly to a 
SharePoint site or OneDrive, or deliver it via a 
password-protected link when data sensitivity 
requires it. That flexibility ensures the recovery 
path adapts to the situation.

Built to meet regulatory requirements
Keepit's data residency options played a 
meaningful role in J.Macêdo's decision. As a 
Brazilian company handling sensitive business 
data, the team needed storage aligned with both 
Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Lei Geral de 
Proteção de Dados, or LGPD) and GDPR 
requirements. Having data centers closer to Brazil 
means better latency for data access and 
restoration, and clearer alignment with local 
regulations.
Immutability was equally important. J.Macêdo's 
audit team needs to access historical data reliably 
and quickly, whether to investigate potential 
misconduct, support compliance programs, or 
review past activity. Keepit's approach, where 
backed-up data and metadata cannot be altered, 
addresses that directly. The company also 
benefits from Keepit's 99-year retention 
capability, a detail Bortoluzzi highlighted 
specifically: "You never know when you'll need to 
restore a specific piece of data."

Data ownership and infrastructure independence 
also factored into the decision. Keepit keeps 
customer data in a separate, dedicated 
infrastructure, meaning J.Macêdo's data is always 
accessible to them, even if something changes in 
their Microsoft environment. 

One platform, room to expand
Bortoluzzi sees J.Macêdo's Keepit footprint 
expanding as the company introduces new SaaS 
applications to its environment. The platform is 
already in place to protect them when that 
happens. For a company that shows no signs of 
slowing down, that kind of readiness is part of how 
J.Macêdo keeps the work going.

About J.Macêdo
With 89 years of history, J.Macêdo is a company from Ceará and one of the largest food companies in Brazil. 
A national leader and reference in the wheat flour and cake mix segments, and one of the most important 
pasta brands in the country, the company also has a presence in the chemical and biological yeast, cookies, 
snacks, and desserts segments. Notable brands include Dona Benta, Sol, Petybon, Brandini, and Boa Sorte. 
In its Professional Line dedicated to the B2B (Business-to-Business) segment, the company maintains a 
portfolio of bread-making and processing flours, bread and cake mixes, flour enhancers, and yeast, among 
others. The company has a vertically integrated and regionally diversified business profile, allowing for 
greater competitiveness and efficiency gains. It has full control of the wheat supply chain, covering all 
stages: wheat purchasing, milling, production, commercialization, and distribution. J.Macêdo is present 
throughout Brazil with Distribution Centers, Administrative and Sales Offices, Mills, and Manufacturing and 
Billing Units.
