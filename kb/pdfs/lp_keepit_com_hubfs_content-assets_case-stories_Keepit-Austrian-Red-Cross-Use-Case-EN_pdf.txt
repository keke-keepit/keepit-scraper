About Austrian Red Cross
The Austrian Red Cross was founded in 1880 as a national institution for humanitarian aid. Its work spans 
emergency medical services, blood donation, health and social services, disaster preparedness and 
response, international development cooperation, dissemination of international humanitarian law, 
migration and tracing services, training and education, and youth work through the Youth Red Cross. 
Organized along federalist lines, the Austrian Red Cross comprises a general secretariat, nine regional 
branches, and 132 district branches, with approximately 10,500 paid staff and more than 81,000 volunteers. 
The global Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement, of which the Austrian Red Cross is a member, has grown 
to 191 national Red Cross or Red Crescent societies operating in 191 countries, with millions of volunteers 
worldwide.
