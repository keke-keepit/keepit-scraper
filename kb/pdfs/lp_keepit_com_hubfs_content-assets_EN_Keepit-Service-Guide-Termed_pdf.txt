1. Introduction 
This service guide describes how Keepit delivers its SaaS data protection services to customers: the 
services and packages available, how usage is measured, the support levels for each package, and 
the commercial terms that apply. It applies to the packages offered to termed customers as described 
on the Keepit pricing page. 
This guide is written for Keepit's direct customers. If you purchase Keepit through an Authorized 
Reseller or Managed Service Provider, your support entitlements and commercial terms are set out in 
your agreement with that partner and may differ from what is described here. 
 
a. Relationship to the Terms of Service 
This service guide is the "Keepit Service Guide" referred to in the Keepit Terms of Service and forms 
part of your Agreement with Keepit. Capitalized terms not defined in this guide have the meaning 
given in the Terms of Service.

2. Definitions 
a. Use Metric & Usage Metering 
Keepit measures usage of each Service and Add-On using a use metric, such as Seats or terabytes. 
Links to the use metric, unit of measurement, and measurement frequency for each Service and Add-
On can be found in Section 4 of this guide. Your Entitlement is the volume of Services and Add-Ons 
stated in your Order Form, measured by the applicable use metric. 
 
b. Base Subscription Fee 
The total recurring fee for the Services on your Order Form, calculated from each Service’s use metric 
(for example, Seats or terabytes) and its unit price, before any Add-On fees are applied. Add-Ons 
priced as a percentage uplift are calculated on this amount. 
3. Services Overview   
Keepit provides Services for backup and recovery for Microsoft 365, Entra ID, and a rapidly growing 
list of popular SaaS applications. A comprehensive list can be found in the table in Section 4, How 
Keepit Meters Usage, below.  
Keepit initiates at least one backup per day for each SaaS Workload unless you configure different 
parameters within the Services, as described in the backup frequency provisions of the Terms of 
Service. The time a backup takes depends on the volume and nature of your data, and Keepit does 
not commit to a backup completion time.

a. Microsoft 365 Fair Usage Policy  
Keepit's Microsoft 365 backup solution provides comprehensive coverage for SharePoint, Groups, 
and Teams, even though the Seats count is based solely on users backed up in Exchange and 
OneDrive. 
To maintain optimal performance and service quality for all customers, the backup of SharePoint and 
Groups & Teams is governed by the following fair usage policy, which is a Use Limitation under the 
Terms of Service. 
The number of users backed up in SharePoint and Groups & Teams is limited to four times (4x) the 
number of Seats backed up in Exchange and OneDrive. If your usage exceeds this limit, Keepit will 
contact you to agree an appropriate adjustment to your subscription. 
5. Commercial and Contractual Terms 
a. Retention 
You can configure retention rules for your data within the Services. Retention is the period a data 
object remains in your backup after it has been deleted from the SaaS Workload. The retention 
included in your subscription is stated in your Order Form, as described in the data retention 
provisions of the Terms of Service. The retention options available depend on your package and any 
retention add-on you have purchased.

c. Termination 
Unless your Order Form states otherwise, your subscription renews automatically for successive 
terms of the same length as the initial term. Either party may terminate by giving notice at least 90 
days before the end of the current term, as set out in the term and termination provisions of the Terms 
of Service. 
To terminate, send written notice to dk.billing@keepit.com or us.billing@keepit.com, as applicable, or 
to your Authorized Reseller if you purchased through one. The notice must include your company 
name, company registration number, contact information, and a statement that you wish to terminate 
the Agreement. 
Termination for material breach is governed by termination for cause provisions of the Terms of 
Service. To expand an existing subscription, contact your Keepit account representative or Authorized 
Reseller.
