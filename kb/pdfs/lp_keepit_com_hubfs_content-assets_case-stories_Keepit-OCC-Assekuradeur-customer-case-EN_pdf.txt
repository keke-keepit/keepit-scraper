Data sovereignty matters
OCC needed its backup data stored in Germany 
to meet EU data protection regulations, 
and Keepit’s Germany data center made that an 
easy decision. 

Keepit’s vendor-independent architecture stores 
backups in a dedicated cloud separate from 
Microsoft’s infrastructure, so the backup stays 
intact and accessible regardless of what happens 
in the primary environment. Immutability and 
encryption met OCC’s internal security standards 
and rounded out the decision. 
Granular restore means recovering exactly 
what is needed 
Since deploying Keepit, the features Jan relies on 
most are the ones that make day-to-day 
operations easier. Easy connectors integrate 
directly with each Microsoft 365 service. And 
granular restore capabilities let the team recover 
exactly what’s needed, whether that’s a single 
email, a SharePoint file, or an entire Teams channel, 
without having to restore everything at once. 

Recovery tests have gone well, giving the IT team 
the confidence that when it matters most, they can 
get their data back.
