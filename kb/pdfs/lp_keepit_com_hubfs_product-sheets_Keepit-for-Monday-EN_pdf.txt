Keepit Backup and 
Recovery for monday.com
Recover lost project and collaboration data instantly  — no disruption
Monday.com holds critical project and operational data that, if lost, impacts productivity and performance. 
Yet, native recovery options are limited to short retention windows. Don’t be mistaken: Protecting your data is 
your responsibility. Accidental deletions, errors, or misuse can permanently erase history, disrupt projects, and 
create compliance risk.
Awarded for its broad SaaS coverage, impenetrable architecture, and ease-of-use, Keepit ensures that your 
monday.com data is backed up and swiftly recoverable at all times, minimizing the impact of data loss and the 
burden of recovery on your teams.
