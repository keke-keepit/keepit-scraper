Customer Story: LAPP
LAPP chooses Keepit 
for critical Azure AD 
coverage and EU 
hosting benefits

On the search for a backup provider, LAPP received a recommendation from 
Keepit partner Syngain GmbH to back up their data with the Keepit solution. 
After a thorough evaluation, LAPP chose Keepit as their trusted backup and 
restore solution following further endorsement from their internal engineers.
Azure AD: The critical need for authentication
Azure AD is a critical application when it comes to receiving authentication 
to other business applications. For this reason, Azure AD backup was a high 
motivator in choosing Keepit. With this backup in place, LAPP can rest assured 
that their authentication provider is backed up, ensuring access to business-
critical data and processes.
—Why is Azure AD backup so critical? In 
case of Azure AD service disruptions or 
data inconsistencies, we would have zero 
authentication to any of our critical business 
applications from today to tomorrow.
Matthias Gut
Head of Digital Innovation & Smart Enterprise at LAPP
—The role of Azure AD has grown 
tremendously in the last 5 years as 
companies increasingly consolidate their 
active directories into this environment.
Dr. Andreas Rebetzky
CEO of Syngain GmbH
EU hosting as a unique selling proposal 
The convenience of EU hosting provided LAPP with an operational advantage. 
This benefit allowed a smooth, easy integration into LAPP’s ecosystem.

Highly recommended by LAPP’s business network
For starters, LAPP trusted their long-term business partner Syngain GmbH in 
their strong recommendation to trust Keepit with their data backup. And when 
two German mid-sized companies in their network recommended Keepit for 
its infrastructure and backup, these positive reviews encouraged them to give 
Keepit a closer look. 
“Two German mid-sized companies in our network 
recommended Keepit for its infrastructure and 
backup.”  
Matthias Gut
Strong, well-organized proof of concept
With LAPP’s engineers on the case, Keepit’s solution was put to the test and 
evaluated for network bandwidth, day-to-day functionality, administration, 
and overall physical infrastructure fit. And after about four weeks of thorough 
evaluation from the engineers, Keepit demonstrated a convincing proof of 
concept. 
For the evaluation, Keepit was evaluated among two other competitors. 
Matthias Gut, Head of Digital Innovation & Smart Enterprise, was pleased to 
see that Keepit was able to back up and recover a large 1GB+ Lotus Notes 
database. These extreme cases were the types of coverage that Matthias was 
looking for.  
LAPP’s engineers also tested the utilization of Keepit’s network bandwidth 
during working (daytime) hours to be sure that it wouldn’t affect any operational 
tasks during daily business operations.  
Finally, Keepit was evaluated for its level of support and technical acumen – 
to make sure that Matthias and his team felt safe and could rely on Keepit in 
potentially challenging situations. 
After this in-depth analysis, the evaluation showed that Keepit could deliver. 
Overall, LAPP evaluated that Keepit could provide an operationally smooth 
integration into their ecosystem.

About LAPP:  Headquartered in Stuttgart, Germany, LAPP is a leading 
supplier of integrated solutions and branded products in the field of cable 
and connection technology. The company’s portfolio includes standard 
and highly flexible cables, industrial connectors and screw technology, 
customised system solutions, automation technology and robotics 
solutions for the intelligent factory of the future, as well as technical 
accessories. LAPP’s core market is in the industrial machinery and plant 
engineering sector. Other key markets are in the food industry, logistics, as 
well as the energy and the mobility sector.  
The company was founded in 1959 and is still fully owned by the founding 
family to this day. In the 2021/22 financial year, it generated a consolidated 
turnover of EUR 1,864 million. LAPP (including its non-consolidated 
companies) currently employs approximately 5,055 people across the 
world, produces at 19 international sites and has over 41 sales companies. 
LAPP also cooperates with around 100 international offices.
