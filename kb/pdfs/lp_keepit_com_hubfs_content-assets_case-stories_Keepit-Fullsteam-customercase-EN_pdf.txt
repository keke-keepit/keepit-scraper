Compliance-driven protection at scale
Fullsteam’s requirements are shaped by scale, 
complexity, and compliance obligations. The 
organization relies on multiple enterprise SaaS 
applications that require comprehensive backups 
to support disaster recovery and precise, targeted 
restores for key business processes. Compliance 
restrictions on where data may be stored made 
regional data residency a critical factor in the 
evaluation process. 
As the team assessed alternatives to its previous 
backup solution, the priorities were clear: scalability 
across multiple SaaS applications, stronger restore 
capabilities, and more granular role-based access 
control within Entra ID and Microsoft 365.

The goal was to delegate backup and restore 
responsibilities within Enterprise IT without 
relying solely on a Global Administrator, while 
maintaining strong governance.
Delegated access with full control
Keepit’s detailed permission structure allowed 
Fullsteam to assign appropriate access across 
multiple layers of the IT organization. That change 
improved operational flexibility while preserving 
control. 
The team also prioritized protecting Entra ID and 
Intune configuration policies to ensure identity 
and device management settings could be 
recovered if needed. 
Fast recovery and continuous data access 
were essential. Even short outages can 
create operational risk, customer impact, and 
compliance exposure. 
Cost and functionality were primary drivers, 
along with the ability to scale beyond Microsoft to 
support additional SaaS platforms over time. The 
evaluation began after Fullsteam’s VP discovered 
Keepit at a technology conference and brought 
it back to the team as a potential replacement 
provider.
Why Fullsteam chose Keepit
Keepit’s US data centers played an important 
role due to compliance requirements restricting 
where Fullsteam data may reside. 
Vendor independence was another key factor. 
Keepit’s architecture supports an independent 
product roadmap, creating opportunities to 
extend backup services to other SaaS platform 
owners within the organization.
Security was equally critical. Encryption 
combined with immutable backups ensures data 
cannot be altered or deleted, preserving reliable 
recovery points.
Keepit’s simple cost structure, including 
unlimited storage, has made spending 
predictable. The solution delivers similar cost to 
the previous platform, with improved usability 
and expanded opportunity to provide backup 
services to other application owners internally.

Independent recovery by design 
Keepit provides a vendor-independent SaaS 
data protection platform purpose-built for 
cloud environments. By storing backups in an 
independent cloud and combining encryption 
with immutability, the platform creates a secure 
foundation for reliable recovery. Flexible access 
controls and scalable architecture enable 
organizations to protect multiple applications while 
maintaining governance and compliance.
For Fullsteam, that means stronger operational 
resilience without added complexity, and confi­
dence that its data is here today and here tomorrow.
