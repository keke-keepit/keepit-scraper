Customer case
ANCA improves data 
protection efficiency and 
compliance with Keepit

Backup failures prompt a rethink 
Before implementing Keepit, ANCA’s IT team 
struggled with its previous backup provider. 
Persistent reliability issues with the previous 
solution put critical data at risk and undermined 
trust in recovery. “The IT team was buried in alerts, 
and we couldn’t guarantee the recovery of critical 
data,” said Andrew Zhou, Group IT Manager at 
ANCA.
With compliance at risk, particularly meeting the 
Essential Eight maturity level 2 requirements, 
the team needed a trusted, transparent solution 
that could deliver consistent backups and fast, 
dependable recovery.
Keepit delivers simplicity, security, and 
compliance 
When it came time to evaluate new options, 
the team had clear priorities: reliability, 
compliance, and ease of use. Working with its 
trusted partner, FullBackup, ANCA discovered 
Keepit as the ideal choice for protecting its 
SaaS data. FullBackup recommended Keepit 
because it provides immutable backups and 
full data sovereignty through its Australian data 
center, both key to meeting ANCA’s compliance 
requirements.
Customer case:
ANCA
When ANCA, an Australian manufacturer known for its precision-engineered CNC grinding 
machines, expanded its use of Microsoft 365, including SharePoint Online for engineering 
documentation and Entra ID for secure identity management, the IT team knew it needed a better 
way to protect critical data. To keep information always accessible and stay compliant with 
Australia’s Essential Eight Maturity Model, ANCA turned to Keepit for a solution that matched its 
values of simplicity, resilience, and innovation.
“Keepit’s immutability and ease of use give 
me total confidence that our data is safe 
and always recoverable.”
-  Andrew Zhou, 
Group IT Manager at ANCA

About ANCA
Founded in 1974 and headquartered in Melbourne, 
ANCA is a world-leading manufacturer of CNC 
grinding machines, automation solutions, and motion 
controls. With customers in more than 45 countries 
and offices across North America, Europe, and Asia, 
ANCA supports industries from automotive and 
aerospace to medical and general manufacturing.
About FullBackup
FullBackup is an Australian data protection 
specialist focused on secure, sovereign, and 
compliant backup solutions. As a trusted Keepit 
partner, FullBackup supports all 12 Keepit-protected 
workloads - including Microsoft 365, Entra ID, 
Google Workspace, Okta, Salesforce, DocuSign and 
more, delivering proven expertise across SaaS 
environments. With a strong local presence and deep 
technical capability, FullBackup helps organizations 
deploy resilient, compliant backup and recovery 
platforms that safeguard data integrity and business 
continuity. 
Deployment was fast and hassle-free. Even 
ANCA’s large SharePoint environment was fully 
protected within days. The platform’s clean, 
intuitive interface meant the team did not need 
extra training to get started. Within moments, they 
had full visibility and control across Microsoft 365 
and Entra ID. 
Reliable protection, improved operations  
With Keepit, ANCA’s IT team no longer has 
to stress about backups. Immutable storage 
keeps data safe from corruption or attack, and 
day-to-day management is simple. The team 
also benefits from smarter recovery tools that 
have improved both efficiency and overall user 
experience across the organization.
End users can now browse snapshots and recover 
what they need on their own, saving IT hours that 
used to be spent chasing vague restore requests. 
Managers also gain instant read-only access 
during employee offboarding, allowing Microsoft 
365 licenses to be retired right away. The result is 
stronger security, lower costs, and more efficient 
operations overall.
Full compliance, renewed confidence 
Since moving to Keepit, ANCA has not had a 
single backup failure and now meets Australia’s 
Essential Eight compliance with confidence. Self-
service recovery has increased efficiency across 
teams by reducing IT workload and providing 
faster access to data when it’s needed most. 
The solution’s reliability and simplicity have 
strengthened resilience against data loss while 
giving ANCA complete assurance in its backup 
strategy.
