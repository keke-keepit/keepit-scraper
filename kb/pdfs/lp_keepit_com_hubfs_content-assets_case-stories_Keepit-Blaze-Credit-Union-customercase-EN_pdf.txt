Streamlining identity and DevOps data 
protection
As SaaS adoption expanded, the team needed 
a way to centrally protect identity and DevOps 
data alongside core productivity applications. 
Managing multiple tools would have added 
operational complexity and increased maintenance 
requirements.
Ease of use, strong restoration capabilities, 
reliability, and low administrative overhead were 
key evaluation criteria. The team also wanted to 
avoid expanding its on-premise footprint while 
ensuring data remained accessible when needed.

One platform to reduce complexity 
Using a single platform to protect multiple 
SaaS applications simplified management 
and eliminated the need for separate backup 
solutions. Consolidation reduced infrastructure 
requirements and allowed the team to manage 
protection more efficiently.  
Keepit’s independent architecture also provided 
additional assurance that backup data would 
remain accessible even if issues affected 
major cloud providers. The availability of U.S. 
data centers offered flexibility should future 
regulatory requirements require data to remain 
within the country. 
Restores that keep teams productive 
Since deploying Keepit, Blaze Credit Union has 
reduced its on-premise footprint and simplified 
its data protection approach. The platform’s 
reliability and ease of use allow the security team 
to focus on broader priorities, confident that 
backup data is consistently protected. 
Fast, reliable restores help support daily 
operations while ensuring important data can be 
recovered when needed.
“Keepit can capture data that no other backup solution has been able 
to capture. That has been extremely beneficial in our day-to-day use, 
especially when restoring files quickly and confidently.”
Grant Aslakson 
Director of Information Security Operations, Blaze Credit Union

A modern approach to multi-application 
SaaS backup 
Blaze Credit Union first learned about Keepit 
through its IT advisory and consulting partner, 
who recommended evaluating the platform. The 
solution aligned closely with the organization’s 
need for simple, dependable SaaS backup that 
supports both operational resilience and long-term 
data protection goals. 
By consolidating protection for productivity, 
identity, and DevOps data into a single platform, 
Blaze Credit Union has implemented a modern 
approach to multi-application SaaS data 
protection that is easy to manage and built for 
resilience.
About Blaze Credit Union
Blaze Credit Union is a full-service financial institution serving 255,000 members across Minnesota through 28 
branches. As the third-largest credit union in the state, Blaze is committed to helping individuals and communities 
thrive by providing competitive financial products, digital banking tools, and local expertise to support saving, 
borrowing, and long-term financial planning.
