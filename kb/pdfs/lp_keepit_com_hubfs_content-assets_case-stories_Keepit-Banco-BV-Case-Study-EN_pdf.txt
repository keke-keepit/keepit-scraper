Cost predictability and performance drove 
the decision
The evaluation process was rigorous. Banco BV 
worked with Movti, a Keepit Value Added Reseller, 
to find a data protection solution that could keep 
pace with its growing environment  and landed 
on Keepit. BV ran a proof of concept designed to 
stress-test whether a solution could meet its 
technical, operational, and regulatory criteria 
simultaneously. Keepit passed. Three factors 
stood out: cost predictability, performance, and 
data security. Unlike models with unpredictable 
scaling costs, Keepit's transparent pricing made 
long-term planning easier and more reliable. The 
absence of additional data transfer costs was a 
practical differentiator in a high-volume 
environment like BV's. 

Keepit's vendor-independent architecture also 
made a strong case. Maintaining a backup copy in 
a completely separate infrastructure  outside 
both Microsoft 365 and BV's own on-premises 
environment  aligned directly with the bank's 
resilience strategy.

Independent infrastructure reinforces 
resilience
Regional data residency also factored into the 
decision. Keepit's regional data centers ensure 
that BV's backed-up data is stored independently. 
That segregation directly supports the bank's risk 
mitigation goals and helps meet the data 
governance requirements of Brazil's financial 
regulators. 

Security features reinforced that confidence. 
Immutability and encryption at the backup layer 
protect data integrity whether BV is dealing with an 
isolated incident or a larger-scale disruption. For a 
bank that treats its backup environment as the last 
line of defense, those protections aren't optional.

Simpler management, faster recovery, 
measurable maturity 
Since implementation, the operational picture at 
BV has improved across several dimensions. 
Backup management is more efficient, reducing 
the administrative overhead that previously came 
with managing data protection at scale.  

Recovery is faster and more granular. The team can 
restore individual emails or files, giving users 
options to recover their own information and 
reducing the time IT spends handling recovery 
requests. More broadly, Bueno's team has seen an 
increase in what he describes as data protection 
maturity at BV 
 greater visibility into protected 
data, stronger governance, and more predictable 
costs. The result is a backup environment the team 
can actually rely on.
