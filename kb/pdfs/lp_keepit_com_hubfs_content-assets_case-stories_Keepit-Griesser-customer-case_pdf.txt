Customer case
Data availability and simplicity: 
Keepit backs up 
Griesser’s critical data

Data availability and retention at the forefront 
“Business continuity is a very important factor 
in production. If production were to come to a 
standstill, it would have far-reaching negative 
consequences,” explains Nico Guglielmo, 
Head of ICT Platform Services at Griesser. The 
company began its search for an independent 
backup solution to secure its data — and comply 
with data retention requirements — when the 
decision to replace its on-premises solution with 
a SaaS solution was made. The Keepit platform 
provides unlimited data storage and retention 
for its customers, allowing companies to not only 
fulfill regulatory requirements, but ensure data 
availability far into the future.
“The manpower required for the old solution was 
around one day’s work per week. This has been 
reduced to almost zero.”
- Nico Guglielmo, 
Head of ICT Platform Services, Griesser
“Thanks to Keepit, we were able to achieve 
a massive increase in data stability and 
availability.”
- Nico Guglielmo, 
Head of ICT Platform Services, Griesser
Customer case:
Griesser
Griesser is a leading provider of high quality and market-responsive solar shading solutions for 
windows and patios in Europe. With European regulations dictating data retention periods, and 
the company seeking an independent backup and recovery provider to cover its 1,750 users, 
Griesser deployed Keepit to secure its Microsoft 356 data, including Entra ID.

About Griesser
In 1882, Anton Griesser laid the foundation stone for 
today’s Griesser Group in Aadorf, Switzerland. The 
company, which is steeped in tradition, has remained 
committed to this location to this day, putting all its 
experience into the manufacture of innovative and 
high-quality sun shading products. The Griesser 
Group is now one of Europe’s leading suppliers of 
high-quality, market-driven sun shading solutions 
for windows and patios. With their commitment and 
enthusiasm, around 1600 employees ensure that the 
excellent reputation of the two independent brands 
Griesser and weinor is confirmed time and time again. 
Griesser and weinor face up to the competition 
independently. Both are united by the common goal of 
providing their customers with a better quality of life. 
Griesser manufactures its wide range of sun shading 
products in Switzerland (Aadorf), Austria (Nenzing) 
and France (Nice and Wolschwiller). Griesser has its 
own companies in five countries and is represented 
by partners in 20 countries. weinor manufactures its 
products for sun and weather protection on patios at 
its main factory in Cologne and in Möckern. weinor is 
active in over 30 countries through its partners.
Simple and easy to use platform   
Choosing Keepit’s platform boiled down to the 
simplicity of the platform. “Handling the backups 
in the platform is very simple and self-explanatory. 
We don’t have to worry about updates, availability, 
maintenance or storage space,” continues 
Nico Guglielmo. With IT resources and budgets 
continuing to be a challenge for most companies, 
Keepit’s solution reduces manual hours spent by 
automating backups, simplifying maintenance 
and ensuring security — all in one simple to use 
platform.
Keepit’s platform streamlines data protection 
with a single, intuitive dashboard that secures 
SaaS applications like Microsoft 365 and 
Salesforce. The platform is seamlessly integrated 
with customer systems and requires almost 
no training, allowing the location, preview, and 
restoration of data with just a few clicks.
Independent data centers in Europe   
Keepit owns and runs its own, independent cloud 
and stores customer data in seven regions across 
the globe. For Griesser, it was paramount to store 
data in Europe. Cloud-native and European born, 
Keepit is completely independent from large 
hyperscalers. “We are convinced that we have 
chosen the right provider,” adds Nico Guglielmo.
