Provides complete coverage for Entra ID - 
and all Microsoft 365 workloads 
After comparing Keepit for Entra ID to 
competitors’ solutions, the team at Örnsköldsvik 
Municipality concluded that Keepit provided 
the best Entra ID coverage on the market. As the 
organization is highly dependent on Entra ID user 
access, any block to this system could bring the 
business to a halt – and extended loss of access 
could have serious impacts. Keepit for Entra ID 
is not only secure and cost-effective but allows 
Örnsköldsvik Municipality to avoid disruption due 
to lost or inaccessible data by enabling instant 
search and comprehensive recovery across Users, 
Groups, Audit Logs, and more.  Additionally, data 
compliance becomes simplified as users can view 
Entra ID at points in the past to quickly see what 
changes have occurred and provide corrections 
where necessary. 
“We are super dependent on Groups in 
Entra ID. Our risk assessments showed that 
a longer duration of access loss could have 
serious impacts. Keepit for Entra ID ensures 
comprehensive recovery of any temporary data 
losses due to a cyberattack or user error.”
- Fredrik Westin 
Technical Supervisor of the Microsoft 365 platform

About Örnsköldsvik 
Municipality:
Örnsköldsvik municipality is the 
municipality’s largest workplace. Just over 
5,000 people in municipal administration 
and municipal companies ensure that the 
municipality’s 56,000 residents receive 
good community services. 
 
The municipality is densely populated 
with many international companies, a long 
entrepreneurial and industrial tradition 
and a strong development focus where 
collaboration is an important key. The 
proximity to Umeå means that Örnsköldsvik 
is part of a larger labor market region in 
Sweden with a total of 200,000 inhabitants 
and 13,000 companies. Örnsköldsvik 
is part of the scenic High Coast where 
archipelago and rocks meet forest and 
wilderness.

Better disaster recovery and data restore 
than Microsoft 
In the current ransomware landscape, a potential 
cyberattack is a matter of “when” – not “if.” With 
a need to recover quickly from a potential cyber 
disaster, Örnsköldsvik Municipality was on the 
lookout for a backup and recovery solution 
that could restore data quickly and ensure that 
business operations keep running as smoothly 
as possible. After doing their own research, 
Örnsköldsvik Municipality learned that Keepit 
offered true, independent, third-party backup. 
And while Microsoft can deliver protection for 
some – but not all – of the organization’s business-
critical data, Keepit came out on top as the better 
workload for coverage, restore granularity, and 
cost-effectiveness. Furthermore, since Keepit 
is independent of Microsoft authentication and 
storage, an attack on the organization’s tenant 
would not endanger backups – meaning that the 
municipality can still reliably access data even 
when Microsoft is having service problems.
“Since using Keepit, we’ve noticed that the data 
restore process is much easier than Microsoft’s. 
Keepit offers independent backup separate to 
Microsoft – ensuring our data is secure even if 
Microsoft service is down.”
- Fredrik Westin 
Technical Supervisor of the Microsoft 365 platform
