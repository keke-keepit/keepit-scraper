Keepit Backup and Recovery 
for Microsoft Applications
A single intelligent platform to protect data in key Microsoft applications
The fast pace of SaaS adoption leads to critical gaps in data protection. Many business 
leaders mistakenly assume that SaaS apps inherently back up all their data. This frequently 
results in costly loss of data and processes, which often can never be recovered.
Built in a sustainable vendor-independent cloud, Keepit securely backs up data from 
multiple Microsoft applications and delivers confidence in your ability to recover fast when 
mistakes or malicious activity lead to data loss. All in a single platform, ensuring full visibility, 
control and compliance with the industry’s best ROI.
Predictable, stable cost

with no storage tiering, zero fees

ingress or egress. You’ll know in 
advance exactly what you’ll pay; today, 
and tomorrow.

applications
