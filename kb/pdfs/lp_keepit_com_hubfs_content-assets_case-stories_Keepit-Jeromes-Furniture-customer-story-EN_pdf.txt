Jerome’s Furniture kept running into the same problem: restores from its old backup service 
failed, in tests and in live recoveries. For a family-owned retailer that’s been in business since 
1954, that wasn’t a risk worth carrying. Adam Warren, senior director of information 
technology, and his team searched until they found Keepit to solve their challenges. Now 
Keepit protects Microsoft 365 and Microsoft Entra ID from a single platform.

One platform now protects Microsoft 365 
and Entra ID  
Keepit replaced the previous service and now 
covers Jerome’s Microsoft 365 tenant and Entra ID. 
The team chose to continue protecting Entra ID 
because it expects its use of Entra and Intune to 
grow. Keepit stores backup data in dedicated 
infrastructure, independent of Microsoft.  
A real-world recovery proved the switch  
A few years ago, IT needed an email from a former 
employee’s mailbox. The mailbox had been lost 
during the account-disabling process, so the 
archived email had to come from backup.  

Email and Microsoft 365 data support day-to-day 
business, and slow recovery can get costly. As 
Warren puts it, reliable backups and restores used 
to be a constant concern. Now Keepit is “a reliable 
part of our IT stack.”

About Jerome’s Furniture
Jerome’s Furniture is a family-owned Southern California furniture retailer in business since 1954. Still owned 
and operated by the Navarra family, the 500-employee company includes six family members, three of 
them third-generation retailers. 
About Vp16 Technologies 
Vp16 Technologies is a disabled veteran-owned, full-service information management and technology 
company. It helps organizations in the U.S. lower the costs, risks, and inefficiencies of managing digital data, 
including backup and archival data. The company also specializes in cybersecurity, data loss protection, and 
awareness training.
