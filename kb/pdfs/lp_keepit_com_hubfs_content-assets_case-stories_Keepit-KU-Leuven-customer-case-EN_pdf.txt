Customer case
KU Leuven ensures 
data sovereignty and 
resilience with Keepit

A solution built for compliance and control  
KU Leuven operates under some of the strictest 
European data protection standards. Handling 
sensitive academic, administrative, and research 
information means the university must maintain 
full control over where its data resides and how it 
is recovered. Keepit’s GDPR-compliant data 
storage in dedicated European data centers met 
this requirement while ensuring complete inde-
pendence from Microsoft’s cloud infrastructure.
Vendor independence was a decisive factor. 
By keeping backups separate from Microsoft’s 
ecosystem, KU Leuven gained an additional 
safeguard against dependency and improved 
control over data retention and recovery.  
Keepit’s platform is built to ensure complete data 
integrity and regulatory alignment. It provides 
an independent, immutable backup environ-
ment with flexible access controls and retention 
policies that fit complex compliance frame-
works. With secure architecture and audit-ready 
transparency, Keepit helps organizations like KU 
Leuven maintain full visibility and control over their 
cloud data — meeting GDPR and other regulatory 
requirements with confidence.
Customer case:
KU Leuven
When one of Europe’s oldest and most respected universities set out to modernize its IT 
infrastructure, data protection was at the top of the agenda. KU Leuven, home to more than 
65,000 students and 12,000 staff, needed to ensure its move to Microsoft 365 would not 
compromise the university’s strict data-sovereignty and GDPR requirements.  
KU Leuven recognized early that Microsoft’s native backup tools were not sufficient for an 
institution of KU Leuven’s scale and compliance demands and therefore launched a public tender 
for a Microsoft 365 backup solution. The European IT solutions provider Cegeka reacted to that 
tender, provided Keepit as the right backup and recovery solution, and guided the university 
through its implementation.
“With Keepit, we’ve gained full confidence 
that our academic and administrative data 
is protected — and that anyone on our 
service desk can perform a restore quickly 
and easily.”
-  Jan De Baere, 
IT manager, KU Leuven ICTS Management Team

About KU Leuven
Founded in 1425, KU Leuven is one of Europe’s leading 
research universities and Belgium’s largest higher 
education institution. The university fosters innovation 
and collaboration across a broad range of disciplines, 
combining centuries of academic tradition 
with cutting-edge research and technology. KU 
Leuven serves more than 65,000 students and 12,000 
staff across its campuses in Leuven and beyond.
About Cegeka
Cegeka is a global technology provider 
headquartered in Hasselt, Belgium, serving more than 
2,500 customers worldwide and has a workforce 
of more than 9,000 employees across Europe, the 
Americas, and Asia. Its comprehensive portfolio 
includes application services, business solutions, 
quality engineering, data and AI, digital workplaces, 
cyber resilience, networking and regulatory, and 
hybrid cloud.
Proven reliability and a future-proof solution    
Today, Keepit safeguards more than 1 petabyte 
of KU Leuven’s OneDrive data in its European 
data centers, with coverage extending across the 
university’s vast Microsoft 365 environment. The 
platform’s fast, automated backups and user-
friendly restore capabilities give the university 
confidence that its data is secure and easily 
recoverable.
Cegeka’s partnership with Keepit provided 
the local support and technical assurance KU 
Leuven needed during the implementation. 
According to Jan De Baere, IT manager, KU 
Leuven ICTS Management Team, the combination 
of Cegeka’s expertise and Keepit’s reliable 
technology created a future-proof solution 
that supports the university’s long-term digital 
transformation goals.
