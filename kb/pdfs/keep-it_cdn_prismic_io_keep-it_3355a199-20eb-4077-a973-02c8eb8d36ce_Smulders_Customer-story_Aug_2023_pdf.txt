Customer Story: SMULDERS
Smulders designates 
Keepit as backup 
of choice for cloud 
migration

With the fast transition to Microsoft Teams during the Covid-19 pandemic, 
Smulders was on the lookout for a cloud-based data backup and restore 
solution to protect their Office 365 data. When Keepit was recommended to 
Smulders by Keepit partner CTG, Smulders evaluated Keepit to explore if it 
could deliver their cloud storage needs. Keepit’s 100% cloud-based solution, 
coupled with its ease of use and cost savings, proved to be the solution they 
were looking for. 
Keepit’s key role in necessary cloud migration
With their employees across the globe working through Microsoft Teams as 
a result of the Covid-19 pandemic, Smulders needed a cloud-based solution 
that could not only back up Teams – but all of their Office 365 data. This cloud-
based backup would secure their data and allow remote team members to 
complete their own restores on their own timeline. 
—Keepit makes our data restore process 
smooth and seamless. Completing a data 
restore in the cloud is much faster than 
doing so on-prem.
Ton Van der Hagen
ICT Hardware & Infrastructure Manager at Smulders
Ease of use and configuration delivers time and resource savings 
Ton Van der Hagen, ICT Hardware & Infrastructure Manager at Smulders, shared 
that their onboarding with the Keepit solution took a mere 2 hours. And now 
that the solution is set up, Ton and his team have peace of mind that Keepit is 
working efficiently in the background while they go about their daily tasks. If a 
team member needs to do a restore or archive a mailbox due to an employee 
departure, they can simply login and complete these tasks in just a few clicks. 
“Keepit just works in the background while we go 
about our daily tasks, saving time and resources. 
The ease of use of this product is a huge strength.”
Ton van der Hagen
Customer Story: SMULDERS

Clear economic benefits compared to competitors 
Having previously stored their Office 365 data with a competitor on-prem, 
Smulders encountered challenges when it came to the price of increasing 
storage. The price of this previous on-prem solution would increase when 
storage needs increased. With Keepit’s cloud-based solution, all data storage 
is unlimited and therefore included – providing an immense benefit to Smulders. 
—In transitioning our data backup from 
on-prem to the cloud, Keepit proved to 
deliver a key benefit in providing unlimited 
storage. With the competitor we worked 
with before, we would have had to pay 
extra for this element – so this provided 
major cost savings for us.
Ton Van der Hagen
ICT Hardware & Infrastructure Manager at Smulders
About Smulders: Smulders is an international steel construction company with more than 1,400 
employees working across offices in Belgium, the Netherlands, Poland and the United Kingdom. 
Active in the Civil & Industry and Offshore Wind markets, the company has a proven track record in 
the engineering, construction, supply and assembly of heavy, technical complex and architectonic 
steel constructions.
