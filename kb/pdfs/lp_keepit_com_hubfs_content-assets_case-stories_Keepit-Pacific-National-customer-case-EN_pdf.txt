Pacific National gains peace 
of mind for the long haul with 
Keepit’s cloud backup solution
Customer case:
Pacific National

Managing the risk of data loss was critical 
Prior to working with Keepit, Pacific National 
carried out a series of risk assessments and 
determined that the native restore tools from 
Microsoft were not as efficient  or user-friendly 
when compared to what Keepit could offer. With 
the conclusion that a major Microsoft 365 cyber 
incident could lead to mass data loss for their 
organization, Pacific National turned to Keepit 
for its independent, third-party backup and 
recovery solution.
Essentially, even if Microsoft is having service 
problems, Pacific National would still be able 
to reliably access their data since all backups 
are separate from Microsoft’s native backup 
solution. With Keepit, their organization can now 
reliably back up Microsoft 365, Microsoft Entra ID, 
Microsoft DevOps, and Microsoft Dynamics 365 – 
and rest assured that their data will be available and 
recoverable even in the event of an unexpected 
cyber event or accidental data deletion.
“The native restore tools from Microsoft are not as 
efficient or user-friendly as Keepit’s backup and 
recovery. We do not want to risk data loss in the 
event of a major Microsoft 365 incident. Keepit’s 
tools save my engineer’s significant time”
- Wilbur Palijo 
IT Infrastructure Manager 
Pacific National
Customer case:
Pacific National
Australia’s largest private rail freight company, Pacific National, chose Keepit to protect their 
business-critical cloud data. In an initiative to ensure that the organization has the most secure 
data backup and recovery solution in place, the organization chose Keepit due to its unparalleled 
independent cloud data coverage (especially the critical access to Microsoft Entra ID) – and the 
promise that the business can always access their data – even in the event of a cyberattack.

About Pacific National:
As Australia’s largest private rail freight 
company, Pacific National is integral in 
keeping Australia’s economy moving. With 
a rich history dating back to 1855, they 
are incredibly proud of their heritage and 
the essential role they continue to play in 
supporting Australia’s supply chain.
The company is built on solid foundations 
with a team of dedicated people, guided by 
common values, working together as one. 
They care about each other and the work 
they do. Because of this, ‘we care more’ is 
the Pacific National core philosophy.
A heavy reliance on access to Microsoft Entra ID 
Pacific National shares that they, like many organizations, 
are immensely dependent on access to Microsoft Entra ID. 
Loss of access would impact business operations, disrupt 
the nations supply chain, and potentially result in financial 
losses. Causing a negative domino effect across other 
cloud applications, Pacific National would face significant 
downtime if access to Microsoft Entra ID were blocked.
The organization chose Keepit for Entra ID due to its ability 
to mitigate accidental or malicious object deletions. The 
solution provides historical records of configuration and 
policy changes. Additionally, an impressive list is protected 
in the Entra ID back up, including: users, devices, groups, 
objects, app registrations, service principals, conditional 
access policies, and compliance policies.
Entra ID holds the key to the organization’s business critical 
data; with Keepit for Entra ID in place, Pacific National can 
rest assured that this data is fully secure and recoverable.
‘Keepit ticks all the boxes’ 
Pacific National evaluated various SaaS backup solutions 
and ultimately chose Keepit due to a range of benefits and 
features provided by the solution. The private rail freight 
company cites ease of use, product maturity, and backup 
frequency as their top reasons for choosing the solution.
A broad array of global backup data centers, dedicated 
technical support, and cost-effectiveness were also 
key factors in selecting Keepit. With seamless product 
maintenance, the solution proved to be a tool that could 
deliver the organization’s needs while being extremely user-
friendly and taking up minimal time resources for the team.
“Fortunately, we have not lost access to Entra ID, 
and we hope that we never do, as the consequences 
would be catastrophic. Backing up our data with 
Keepit for Entra ID ensures that we never lose 
access to this business-critical data – which 
provides our organization with peace of mind.”
- Tony Azzi 
Senior Infrastructure Engineer 
Pacific National
