Purpose
Choosing a SaaS backup and recovery solution often comes down to more than just features 
— it’s about trust, reliability, and long-term data protection strategy. 
This report provides a side-by-side comparison of Keepit and alternative SaaS backup 
solutions based on verified user feedback from G2. It is designed to help IT and security 
teams evaluate how platforms perform in real-world environments, across usability, support, 
and overall satisfaction.
About G2
G2 is the world’s largest and most trusted software marketplace. More than 90 million 
people annually — including employees at every Fortune 500 company — use G2 to make 
informed software decisions based on peer reviews. 
To ensure credibility, G2 verifies reviewers through LinkedIn or business email validation, and 
all reviews are moderated before publication. Rankings and scores reflect authentic user 
experiences and publicly available data. 
Data note: All data in this report is sourced from the G2 SaaS Backup category and reflects available 
metrics from G2 Spring 2026 Reports. Metrics shown may vary by vendor based on review volume and data 
availability.
