Riverstone Logistics moves big and bulky freight across complex supply chains for 
manufacturers, retailers, home builders, and national brands. That kind of operation depends 
on reliable technology, and as the company’s Microsoft 365 footprint grew, so did a critical 
question: what happens to business-critical data if something goes wrong? Protecting it 
from accidental deletion, ransomware, insider threats, or operational disruptions wasn’t just 
an IT concern — it was a business continuity requirement. Riverstone needed an independent 
backup and recovery solution that could stand apart from the Microsoft environment, align 
with its long-term security strategy, and scale without adding management overhead.

Meeting customers where they are
From the first conversation, the Keepit 
team took time to understand Riverstone’s 
environment, recovery goals, and growth plans 
before recommending a path forward. Rather 
than applying a fixed approach, they worked 
to identify the licensing and configuration that 
made the most sense for where Riverstone 
was and where it was headed. That kind of 
consultative engagement is central to how Keepit 
approaches new customer relationships.  
The implementation reflected the same 
spirit. Onboarding was straightforward, and 
Riverstone’s team came away understanding 
not just how to use the platform but why it 
was built the way it was. That foundation 
matters: Keepit’s immutable-by-design 
architecture means backups are stored in a 
resilient, independent cloud with an air-gapped 
design, so data can’t be altered or deleted, giving 
Riverstone reliable recovery points regardless of 
what caused the disruption.  
Disaster recovery readiness, by design
Since deploying Keepit for Microsoft 365 
and Entra ID, Riverstone Logistics has gained 
something that’s hard to put a number on: 
confidence. The team now has independent 
backup protection for the collaboration and 
identity services that keep the business running. 
Recovery processes are simpler, business 
continuity planning is on stronger footing, 
and executives have greater visibility into the 
organization’s overall resilience posture.
For Riverstone, Keepit has become a core 
part of a broader cyber resilience strategy, 
supporting ongoing work around security 
maturity, compliance readiness, and operational 
continuity.  
“The onboarding experience felt more like a partnership than a vendor

Michelle Graham

About Riverstone Logistics
Riverstone Logistics partners with manufacturers, retailers, home builders, contractors, and national brands to 
move big and bulky goods across the full supply chain. From middle mile to active job site and in-home delivery, 
Riverstone brings the coverage, capacity, and coordination complex freight requires.
