Customer case
Keepit and freenet: 
Resilience in the face 
of cyber threats

Protection against ransomware attacks 
When searching for a data protection partner, 
freenet’s focus was on reliably preventing data 
loss or manipulation and ensuring full data 
recovery when needed. The Keepit solution 
runs on a vendor-independent, tamper-proof, 
and air-gapped cloud infrastructure, making it 
uniquely strong against ransomware and other 
cyberthreats.
Customers, such as freenet, have ensured access 
to stored data in the event of a system failure or a 
compromised user account. “We’ve found Keepit 
to be an effective and reliable tool to secure 
Microsoft Office 365 and Microsoft Entra ID data 
— safeguarding its IT infrastructure against threats 
such as ransomware attacks,” remarks Andre 
Quandt, Head of Workplace Services at freenet.
Ensuring business continuity 
With ransomware attacks becoming more 
prevalent, the Keepit platform limits the impact 
of data breaches by allowing quick recovery, 
preventing data loss, and reducing downtime 
— and ultimately ensuring business continuity. 
As backups are a prime target for attackers, 
with 99% of ransomware incidents attempting 
to compromise them, implementing security 
measures like air-gapping, immutability, and 
encryption fortifies freenet’s cyber resilience.
“One of our primary objectives is to prevent 
scenarios in which Microsoft Office 365 user 
data is encrypted and rendered inaccessible due 
to attacks, or where Entra ID accounts and tenant 
configurations are compromised.”
- Andre Quandt, 
Head of Workplace Services at freenet
Customer case:
freenet
freenet, the largest network-independent telecommunications provider in Germany, 
required comprehensive data security measures to protect its Microsoft Office 365 and 
Microsoft Entra ID tenants from data loss scenarios. To achieve this, freenet worked with 
trusted partner IT sure to choose Keepit’s backup and recovery solution to secure the data of 
approximately 3,200 employees and ensure uncomplicated restoration.

About freenet
freenet sees itself as a digital lifestyle provider. Digital 
lifestyle mainly includes telecommunications, TV, 
internet and mobile devices as well as all services, 
applications and equipment associated with mobile 
devices or that can be controlled or used via an 
intelligent device. With a business model consistently 
geared towards customers and their needs, freenet is 
one of Germany’s leading market players with around 
9,5 million subscribers.
About IT sure
IT sure is an established partner of Microsoft and 
Keepit and help medium-sized companies to 
maximize their competitive advantage through the 
efficient use of the Microsoft Cloud. As a Keepit Elite 
Partner, IT sure focuses on modern data protection 
in the cloud — GDPR-compliant, transparent and 
independent.
With IT sure’s services and managed cloud services 
around Microsoft Azure, Microsoft 365 and Microsoft 
Fabric, it increases IT security, communication, 
collaboration and data culture for its customers. IT 
sure can look back on over 20 years of experience in IT 
consulting. 
Versatility and intuitive operation as benefits   
“Keepit stood out to us due to its ease of use and 
the capability to back up not only standard Office 
365 data, but also specific content such as Teams 
tabs, apps, and Entra ID data. This versatility 
and intuitive operation were decisive factors in 
choosing Keepit,” said Andre Quandt, Head of 
Workplace Services at freenet.
