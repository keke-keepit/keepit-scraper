Keepit and the French Tennis Federation: 
A grand slam for 
data protection

The search for vendor-independent backup 
Realizing that Microsoft would not be responsible 
for recovering lost data, the French Tennis 
Federation began to search for a vendor-
independent backup and recovery solution 
that would also be easy to implement, agile, 
and secure. “At the time, we had no backup for 
cloud-based applications, and my focus was 
to find a European backup solution that was as 
independent as possible. I did some research 
and found a company called Keepit in Denmark,” 
explains Frank Labat, IT Technical Director at the 
French Tennis Federation. 
“Keepit has brought us what we initially wanted 
— independent backup. It has also brought us 
quite specific uses that we hadn’t identified at 
the beginning, such as centralized and traceable 
archiving and the ability to integrate everything 
into the same platform.”
- Frank Labat, 
IT Technical Director at the French Tennis Federation
Customer case:
French Tennis Federation
The French Tennis Federation, tasked with the organization, coordination, and promotion of the 
sport, chose Keepit to independently back up its Microsoft 365, Microsoft Entra ID, and Power 
BI data. Since 2022, the federation, headquartered at the famous Roland Garros stadium, has 
trusted Keepit to secure its data — facilitating important work developing tennis through 8,000 
tennis clubs across the country. The partnership was made possible by SCC France, a long-
standing partner of the federation.

About the French Tennis Federation
The French Tennis Federation (French: Fédération 
française de tennis, FFT) is the governing body for 
tennis in France. It was founded in 1920, and is tasked 
with the organization, coordination and promotion of 
the sport. It is recognized by the International Tennis 
Federation and by the French Minister for Sports. Its 
headquarters are at the Roland Garros stadium in 
France.
Keepit owns and operates its own cloud and data 
centers, making sure backed-up data is always 
online, even in cases of vendor downtime. This 
was exactly what the French Tennis Federation 
was looking for — backing up their data in an 
environment separated from their production 
environment.
Expected and unforeseen benefits  
Due to the nature of its operations, the French 
Tennis Federation deals with a lot of seasonal 
employees. This means a lot of arrivals and 
departures, and more data to process — and 
recover. Keepit helps to facilitate this process 
while ensuring the security of the data being 
handled.
Continued partnership 
A relationship that started with backing up 
Microsoft 365 has grown into a well-established, 
developing partnership in which the federation 
has integrated Power BI and Microsoft Entra ID 
with the Keepit platform.
“There are many new developments in the pipeline 
for Microsoft. The plan is that every time we 
add new assets to Microsoft 365, they can be 
integrated with Keepit,” says Frank Labat.
