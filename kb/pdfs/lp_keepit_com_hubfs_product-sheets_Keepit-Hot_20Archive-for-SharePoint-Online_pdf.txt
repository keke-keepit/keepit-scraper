Why purchase this add-on?
SharePoint Online quietly becomes one of the biggest line items in your Microsoft 365 bill. Sites multiply, files 
pile up, and soon you're paying production prices for data nobody opens. Most can't be deleted, so it stays in 
production, driving up storage costs. Traditional archiving fixes the cost but buries data in cold storage, adding 
friction right when you need it back.
You can now purchase this add-on to Keepit for Microsoft 365 to effectively reduce storage costs while 
maintaining full access to sites and files.
