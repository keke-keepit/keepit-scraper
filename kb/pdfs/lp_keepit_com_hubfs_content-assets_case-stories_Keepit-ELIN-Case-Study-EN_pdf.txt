Customer case
Keepit provides 
cost-effective backup 
for ELIN Group

Easy-to-use, cost-effective backup 
ELIN Group’s previous backup and recovery 
solution was complicated to use, and the 
company, alongside partner LB-systems, went 
searching for a new, more user-friendly option. 
“Our old solution was very unhandy, and the user 
interface was terrible. We wanted an intuitive to 
use, cost-effective solution and were introduced 
to Keepit,” says Alfred Pusterhofer, Head of IT at 
ELIN Group.  
With Keepit, ELIN Group can recover critical 
Microsoft 365 data in seconds. The intuitive 
interface requires minimal to no training, allowing 
customers to locate, preview, and restore data 
with just a few clicks. “The ability to send backup 
data to our users through a web link has been very 
helpful to us in our daily work,” adds Alfred.
Customer case:
ELIN Group
ELIN Group, an electrical engineering and technical building services company in Austria, 
searched for a new solution to back up its Microsoft 365 environment. With data retention 
requirements of up to 35 years, the company needed a provider that could offer a cost-effective 
and easy-to-use solution to comply with these requirements and secure its 1,800 users’ data.
“European data centers and immutability 
were important to us when choosing a 
backup and recovery vendor.”
- Alfred Pusterhofer, 
Head of IT at ELIN Group

About ELIN Group
ELIN is Austria’s leading company in building services 
and electrical systems construction, providing both 
comprehensive system-integrative and specialized 
engineering solutions. With 17 dedicated Centers 
of Competence, ELIN consolidates deep technical 
know-how and continuously advances innovation—
also developing proprietary products (for example in 
airport technology). The company generates around 
€415 million in annual revenue, has around 1,800 
employees, and maintains 15 locations in Austria and 6 
branches internationally (www.elin.com).
About LB-systems
For almost 40 years now, LB-systems has been 
involved with the topic of data and its availability. 
From the very beginning, its focus has been on 
technology and its in-house technical expertise. LB-
systems delivers solutions that scale with customers’ 
increasing demands – simple, cost-effective, and 
secure (www.lb-systems.at).  
Focus on securing business continuity     
Business continuity was important to ELIN Group, 
and when looking for a backup and recovery 
partner, they highlighted the importance of 
security measures such as immutability. “If our 
systems crash, we in IT must be up and running 
again as soon as possible. And to achieve this, 
the backup of Microsoft 365 using Keepit plays a 
crucial role,” explains Alfred. 
With increasing regulations, companies are 
expected to have disaster recovery plans in place 
— Keepit easily secures business critical data to 
enable companies to bounce back quickly in case 
of any data loss incident.
Vendor-independent backup and recovery 
European-born Keepit provides completely 
independent backup for companies looking 
to secure their critical data. With data centers 
globally, customers can store their data in their 
own region to ensure data sovereignty. “When you 
follow the current geopolitical situation, European 
data centers are increasingly important to us,” 
says Alfred.
The intelligent approach to compliance 
Keepit’s platform prioritizes cloud data integrity 
and compliance, providing an independent, 
immutable backup environment with flexible 
access control and retention options. From 
secure architecture to audit-ready logs, Keepit 
ensures data remains protected, transparent, 
and aligned with all regulatory requirements.
