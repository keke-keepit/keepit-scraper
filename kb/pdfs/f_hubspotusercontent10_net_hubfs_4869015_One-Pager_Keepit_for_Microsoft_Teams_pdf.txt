Top Reasons Why Microsoft Teams Needs Backup:
Businesses often overlook key facts that put Teams data at risk:   
•	 Microsoft is not responsible for backing up Teams data; if Microsoft infrastructure suffers 
an outage or attack, you will not have access to (or may even lose) your data. 
•	 Without a strong link to SharePoint, Teams backups are very limited since Teams data 
is located on and restored from the SharePoint team site. Your backup and recovery 
solution must have this connection.  
•	 More collaboration = greater chance of human error. Teams users can easily overwrite 
and delete data, oftentimes accidentally. Your risk for data loss increases with 
collaboration. 
Teams brings in data that can be edited or deleted from many sources in your tenant: Be 
proactive and don’t let collaboration become a source of costly business disruption. To 
always be in control of your data, you need a true backup and recovery solution on a private 
infrastructure.
