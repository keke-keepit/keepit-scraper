Navigating complex compliance  
A multinational financial services provider, Saxo 
Bank is subject to stringent compliance and 
regulatory control across its operations world-
wide. This in turn requires an efficient search func-
tion that enables Saxo Bank to find and share 
specific data with its legal team within minutes. 
Keepit’s unique find and restore features 
facilitate this.
Extensive archiving capability 
It was key for Saxo Bank to have backup func-
tionality outside of the Microsoft ecosystem and 
meet compliance requirements as they do for Cis-
co phones and Skype for Business today. Keep-
it’s solution not only safeguards emails and files 
for Saxo Bank’s 1,600+ global employees world-
wide, it also provides the ability to rapidly gain 
access to any piece of backed-up information. 
Customer case:
Saxo Bank
In 2019, Saxo Bank implemented Keepit as the backup and recovery service for all employees 
on Microsoft 365. Saxo Bank has recently implemented Microsoft Teams and plans to introduce 
more features going forward in collaboration with Keepit as the trusted provider, protecting their 
cloud SaaS data.
“An impressive element of  Keepit’s solution 
is that it often takes only minutes to find and

whilst it often took much longer with our on-
prem solution.”
-  Thomas Ulrich 
Head of Digital Workplace & Support Services 
Saxo Bank

Keepit’s customer-focused services met all of 
Saxo Bank’s needs and process requirements 
from the very start. As Saxo Bank continues to 
implement more Microsoft 365 features, Keepit 
will continue to safeguard their cloud SaaS data.
About Saxo Bank
Saxo Bank is a regulated bank offering financial 
services worldwide. The Copenhagen-based financial 
organization has over 860,000 clients and 120 partner 
banks that use Saxo Bank’s platforms to place over 
125,000 trades a day at industry-leading prices.  As a 
fintech bank, Saxo Bank launched one of the industry’s 
first online trading platforms, making global markets 
available to everyone. 
The company is currently investing heavily in new 
technology to empower clients with institutional-
grade execution and powerful features.
