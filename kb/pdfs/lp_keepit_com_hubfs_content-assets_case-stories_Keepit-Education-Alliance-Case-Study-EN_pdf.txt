Value won the evaluation
When their existing contract came up for renewal, 
TEAL was already looking for a change. The priority 
was a clean, easy-to-navigate interface with a 
straightforward onboarding process. The 
evaluation focused on what would matter most in a 
real recovery scenario: ease of use, cost 
predictability, reliability, and the assurance that 
backed-up data couldnˇt be altered or deleted. 
 
The procurement process stood out, too. The 
Keepit team took time to understand TEALˇs goals 
and show how the platform addressed them. Cost 
matters in education, but value was the real 
measure, and Keepit delivered on both. 

GDPR made data residency non-
negotiable
For a UK education institution handling data about 
children, knowing exactly where that data lives is a 
legal requirement. Under Article 35 of UK GDPR, 
organizations in this position must complete a 
Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA), a 
process that requires knowing precisely where 
data is stored and which jurisdiction governs it. 
 
Working through the DPIA with the Keepit team 
helped TEAL establish data residency 
requirements more quickly than anticipated. 
Keeping backups within European data centers,
independent of the Microsoft and Google tenants 
but subject to UK and EU data protection law, gave 
the trust clear, demonstrable control over where 
its data resides.

Immutability gives the IT team confidence
When you implement a backup procedure, you're 
already planning for the worst. For Wadsworth, the 
question is what happens if that worst case 
extends to the backup itself. A compromised 
backup doesn't just fail to help; it makes an already 
bad situation significantly worse. 
 
Keepit stores backed-up data in an independent, 
vendor-neutral cloud, logically separated from the 
Microsoft and Google environments it protects. 
That data can't be altered or deleted by anyone, 
including a threat actor who has already breached 
the production environment. For TEAL, that 
separation is the foundation of the whole data 
protection strategy. 
 
"To imagine someone breaching your backup too 
makes the situation ten times worse," says 
Wadsworth. "Knowing the data is immutable gives 
you the confidence to know the data is safe." 

Faster recovery, less friction 
In day-to-day use, Keepit earns its place well 
outside a crisis. Recovery is faster and simpler than 
with previous systems. Tasks like recovering a 
deleted email folder or reviewing data from a 
specific point in time can now be completed in a 
few clicks.

About The Education Alliance 
The Education Alliance (TEAL) is a Multi-Academy Trust made up of 12 schools in Yorkshire: four secondary 
schools, seven primary schools, and an alternative provision for key stages 2 to 4. TEAL opened as a trust in 
2015 with one secondary school and has grown steadily since. It now serves 7,000 young people across 
East Yorkshire, Hull, and York and employs around 1,000 people. TEAL is deeply committed to delivering 
inclusive, high-quality education so that all young people feel they belong and can achieve and thrive.
