For SPIEGEL-Verlag, digital growth has expanded how content is created, shared, and 
protected across the organization. While the company continues to publish its well-known 
print magazines – such as DER SPIEGEL that is one of the largest news publications in Europe – 
its growing portfolio of online platforms and digital services has increased the importance of 
ensuring business-critical data remains secure and recoverable. Protecting SaaS application 
data is essential to supporting daily operations and maintaining continuity across editorial 
and business teams. 
Miroslav Najda, head of IT Operations and DevOps, is responsible for the company’s IT 
architecture and security, ensuring systems remain resilient and available to support 
SPIEGEL’s evolving digital environment.

Multi-application SaaS coverage from one 
platform
As the SaaS environment at SPIEGEL-Verlag 
expanded, the IT team began evaluating how 
to back up Atlassian products, Microsoft Power 
Platform, and other business-critical SaaS 
applications. Existing tools did not provide 
consistent coverage across these platforms, 
prompting the team to reassess how they 
approached SaaS data protection. 
Trusted technology partner Serviceware introduced 
Keepit as a platform capable of protecting a 
broad range of SaaS applications. Serviceware 
supported SPIEGEL-Verlag throughout the process 
by setting up and conducting the proof of concept, 
followed by the implementation of the solution 
and its transition into live operations. What began 
as a search to address specific gaps evolved into a 
decision to centralize protection for Microsoft 365, 
Atlassian products, Salesforce, Microsoft Power 
Platform, and Entra ID within one solution.

Fast, reliable restores keep teams 
productive 
SPIEGEL-Verlag’s IT organization delivers 
backup and recovery as an internal service 
across departments, ensuring teams can 
quickly recover lost files or data when needed. 
Restore capabilities are critical to maintaining 
productivity and avoiding disruption.
Granular recovery options allow the IT team to 
efficiently restore data and support internal users 
without complex or time-consuming processes.
German data storage for greater control

organizational requirements.

About SPIEGEL Group
The SPIEGEL Group based in Hamburg is one of the most respected German media houses. Print magazines, digital 
offerings and TV productions enjoy an outstanding position in the German media landscape. The exceptional jour-
nalistic quality and independent reporting form the core of all journalistic products.
In addition to SPIEGEL, Germany’s most important and Europe’s largest news magazine, the business magazines 
manager magazin and Harvard Business manager also belong to the SPIEGEL Group, as does the SPIEGEL TV brand, 
which produces video content for all distribution channels. 11FREUNDE, the magazine for football culture, and Ef-
filee, the culinary culture magazine, round off the diverse range.
In 2024, the SPIEGEL Group generated sales of 255 million euros with around 1400 employees. In Germany, the 
group has nine editorial offices. Internationally, it is represented at around 20 locations.
