Identity powers access. Recovery keeps it running.
Identity and access management systems like Microsoft Entra ID and Okta are now central to modern IT 
environments, acting as gatekeepers to critical applications, data, and workplace tools. If users cannot 
authenticate, they cannot access platforms like Microsoft 365 — and productivity stops.
Yet many organizations focus on protecting identities without planning for recovery. When something 
goes wrong, those identities must be restored quickly and reliably. According to the Keepit Annual Data 
Report 2026, identity systems are tested four times less often than productivity systems.
Keepit provides independent, resilient backup and recovery for identity systems, ensuring you can 
restore access quickly and maintain control when it matters most. Here’s how Keepit addresses the key 
challenges of identity protection:
Identity protection 
with Keepit
Identity systems are highly interconnected, meaning even small changes — whether accidental deletions, 
misconfigurations, or malicious actions — can cascade into widespread access issues. Keepit enables fast, 
complete recovery of your identity environment with granular, point-in-time precision. Restore individual 
objects or entire tenants, and recover to a secondary tenant if needed, minimizing downtime and ensuring 
continued access to critical applications. 
Identity continuity and recovery
Keepit’s solution
The challenge: Loss of identity control and identity downtime can 
escalate from minor errors into widespread access disruptions.
Many identity and access management (IAM) platforms like Entra ID and Okta lack true backup and point-
in-time recovery, offering only partial features like soft deletion or manual rebuilds after misconfigurations. 
At the same time, relying on the same environment for both identity services and data protection creates a 
single point of failure. Keepit delivers comprehensive coverage with snapshot-based backups stored in an 
independent cloud, ensuring resilient, granular recovery even when native safeguards fall short. 
Independent, comprehensive identity backup and recovery
Keepit’s solution
The challenge: Limited backup and recovery capabilities in identity 
platforms create gaps that increase risk and slow recovery.

Regulations like GDPR and FINRA require organizations to enforce strict control over access to sensitive 
data and maintain auditable records of user activity. Identity systems define who can access which 
resources and under what conditions, but without a recoverable history, proving this during audits, 
investigations, or insurance claims becomes difficult. Keepit provides immutable backups with unlimited 
retention, ensuring a verifiable record of identity configurations and policies over time, enabling 
organizations to demonstrate compliance with confidence.  
Ensuring compliance and audit readiness
Keepit’s solution
The challenge: Failure to meet compliance requirements when identity 
policies and access controls cannot be historically verified.
As organizations restructure IAM to support agentic AI, access decisions become faster, more dynamic, 
and increasingly autonomous. This creates a new risk: identity misconfigurations and access abuses 
can propagate at machine speed. Keepit provides a resilience layer for Entra ID and Okta, enabling 
organizations to quickly recover from misconfigurations, AI-driven access abuse, or compromised 
identities. By restoring a trusted identity state, it limits blast radius, ensures continuity, and enables secure, 
scalable adoption of agentic AI. 
Resilient identity protection for AI-driven environments
Keepit’s solution
The challenge: Securing and restoring identity and access in the age of 
agentic AI.
While identity platforms like Entra ID and Okta are critical, they are only the gateway to a broader SaaS 
ecosystem. Without consistent protection across applications like Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, 
Salesforce, and others, organizations risk data loss and operational disruption beyond identity. Keepit 
delivers unified, comprehensive backup across identity and SaaS platforms, ensuring consistent 
protection and recovery across your entire cloud environment. 
Protecting your entire SaaS ecosystem
Keepit’s solution
The challenge: Protecting identity alone leaves critical SaaS apps 
exposed, increasing business risk.
Protect identities and everything they unlock
Identity is the control plane of your business. When it fails, access stops, operations stall, and risk 
spreads quickly. Protecting identity without a recovery strategy leaves a critical gap. 
Keepit helps close that gap. With independent, comprehensive backup and recovery for identity 
and SaaS applications, you can restore what matters — fast, precisely, and on your terms. 
From everyday errors to complex incidents, you maintain control, limit disruption, and support
