Microsoft Azure DevOps
 Protect and swiftly recover Microsoft Azure DevOps data for business continuity
Lose Microsoft Azure DevOps data and you’ve lost your software development brain and 
nervous system. Under the shared responsibility model, Microsoft ensures the availability 
of the platform, but your data is your responsibility. Without proactive backups or disaster 
recovery strategies, the smallest mistake or malicious act can severely cripple your 
organization’s software development lifecycle. 
Awarded for its impenetrable architecture and ease-of-use, Keepit ensures that your data 
remains accessible and can be recovered swiftly and accurately at all times. Keepit effectively 
minimizes the impact of data loss and the burden of recovery on your teams.
