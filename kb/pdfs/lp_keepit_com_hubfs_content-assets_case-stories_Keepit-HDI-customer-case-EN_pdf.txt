Customer case:

Nonstop availability at the center of compliance 
As an insurance company, HDI handles sensitive 
data that is overseen by some of the toughest 
regulations in Europe, including the Digital 
Operational Resilience Act (DORA). “In Germany, 
for example, we need to have 24/7 availability 
as we’re considered critical infrastructure and 
regulated under BaFin and DORA; insurance 
fees need to be paid out in the case of a crisis,” 
explains Philipp Poppe from HDI.   
Keepit’s solution can provide HDI with needed 
confidence that all backups will be available, if 
needed. Other features such as immutability by 
default and granular recovery make it easier to 
comply with any and all regulations and to provide 
the necessary documentation for audits.
Keepit’s independent cloud and data centers 
as a differentiator  
Keepit is the world’s only vendor-independent 
cloud for SaaS data protection. With data centers 
in seven regions, including Europe, customers can 
be sure of both data security and sovereignty.
Keepit Backup for Microsoft Entra ID gives 
companies like HDI visibility into their data 
by detecting what changes have occurred 
and allowing them to correct them quickly— 
something an insurance provider like HDI requires 
to keep its systems compliant.
“We have the highest data protection needs 
as we handle live data, personal data for life 
insurances, and private insurances. We have only 
a very short time range to get “back to live” in 
case of a disaster.”
- Philipp Poppe, 
HDI Cloud, Product Manager Core Services
Customer case:
HDI
Insurance solutions provider HDI handles some of the most sensitive personal data available 
in its operations, making it critical for it to choose a backup and recovery partner it can trust. 
Keepit ensures HDI’s Microsoft Entra ID is not only securely backed up but also compliant with 
regulations such as the Digital Operations Resilience Act (DORA).
“Keepit has their own data centers, so we 
are completely non-reliant on Microsoft 
Entra ID in case of service outages.”
- Niko Pieper, 
HDI Cloud Core Services

About HDI:
HDI VVaG offers property insurance solutions for 
private and corporate customers, as well as special 
solutions for the self-employed. The company is part 
of the Retail Germany division of the Talanx Group. 
With insurance revenue of EUR 43.2 billion (2023) and 
approximately 28,000 employees, Talanx is one of 
Europe’s largest insurance groups. Talanx AG is listed 
on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange in the MDAX and on 
the Hanover Stock Exchange. 
Uncomplicated procurement process 
In addition to the benefits of Keepit’s 
platform, HDI also appreciated the swift 
and uncomplicated cooperation during the 
procurement process. As a backup and recovery 
partner is considered critical outsourcing, HDI 
needed to be sure its chosen provider could meet 
their high expectations.
“We chose to do a proof of concept in our 
procurement and ended up with Keepit after 
testing multiple solutions. The discussions 
around the contract and the purchase with 
both Keepit and our joint partner Bechtle were 
very cooperative, which helped us to reach 
some timelines, which were tight,” told Philipp 
Poppe, and continued: “Very fast replies and 
easy discussions were also needed for our 
procurement department, which isn’t always that 
easy, especially at a German insurance company.”
