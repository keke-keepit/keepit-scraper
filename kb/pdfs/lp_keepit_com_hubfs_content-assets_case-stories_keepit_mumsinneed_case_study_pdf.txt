Mums In Need secures 
sensitive Google Workspace 
data with Keepit 
Customer case:
Mums In Need

A charity looking to secure its Google 
Workspace data 
Mums In Need is on a mission to help women 
rebuild their lives after experiencing domestic 
abuse. This Sheffield-based charity supports 
mothers affected by emotional abuse and post-
separation abuse in South Yorkshire and the 
surrounding area.
As Laura Riley, CEO at Mums In Need, laid out their 
requirements for data protection, it became clear 
that a cloud-based backup solution was essential. 
The entire organization stores its data in the cloud, 
using Google Workspace with their volunteers, 
trustees, ambassadors and patrons who log into 
the platform from various locations across the 
United Kingdom.
Keepit is a set-and-forget solution, and 
everything is available from one screen - so we 
can check how much data is being backed up and 
ensure everything is running smoothly.”
- Laura Riley, CEO at Mums In Need
Customer case:
Mums In Need
Simplicity, peace of mind and compliance were top of mind for Mums In Need when choosing 
a backup and recovery provider. As a small organization without a specialized IT staff, Keepit’s 
solution was the perfect choice for securing sensitive data in Google Workspace.

About Mums In Need:
Mums In Need (MIN) is a Sheffield-based 
registered charity working to confront 
coercive control. They support mothers 
in Sheffield and the wider South Yorkshire 
area who have left an abusive relationship 
and are looking for help and guidance to 
rebuild their lives.  
Mums In Need provides practical guidance 
and emotional support so their clients can 
gain an understanding of exactly what 
they can do to help their families. Mums In 
Need empower women with the skills and 
confidence to move forward with their 
lives, and is working towards being a charity 
with national reach in the UK.
Keepit’s “set-and-forget” solution was the right fit 
Mums In Need looked for a solution that would fulfill the 
requirements of a small organization without specialized 
IT staff – Keepit was the right fit. “We looked for a service 
that could protect cloud data. After reviewing players 
in that market, we settled on Keepit because it met our 
requirements most closely. It’s a backup service that 
protects our cloud data and makes it easy for us to restore it, 
should we need to,” says Laura Riley.
Sensitive data requires backup you can trust 
A charity such as Mums In Need handles sensitive data 
related to personal matters. It was vital for the organization 
to have full control over the data they process and store.
Independent data centers to ensure compliance 
When looking at criteria for a backup and recovery service, 
the fact that Keepit owns and operates its own data 
centers was a clear advantage. Keepit has data centers in 
seven regions to ensure that customers comply with local 
regulations.
“Data is the heart of our organization. We must 
retain the trust of the women using our service and 
Keepit is critical to this process.”
“We are confident that our data is 100% protected 
and compliant.”
- Laura Riley, CEO at Mums In Need
- Laura Riley, CEO at Mums In Need
