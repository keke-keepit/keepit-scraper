Krona revolutionizes security 
and data protection of their 
Google Workspace with  
Keepit and IT partner Movti
Customer case:
Krona

Recently, Krona went through a process of 
improving its internal information security, thanks 
to its partnership with Movti and the adoption of  
Keepit’s backup and recovery services.  
Krona’s cybersecurity challenges  
Even though the team at Krona was aware of the 
security resources offered in products it was 
already using, the company was experiencing 
difficulty with the information retention period the 
former plans were offering. 
In other words, keeping these records for longer 
than necessary was an issue that left much to be 
desired at the time. 
The reality of not having a unified, easily 
accessible platform for making fast backups and 
displaying information was also something the 
company’s IT team was missing. 
Customer case:
Krona
Consulting with Movti was key to success  
At this time, Movti was already Krona’s partner in 
the Google Workspace project, which was also 
a success story for deploying and adopting the 
solution. 
Gilharde Wasneski, Krona’s Information 
Architecture and Security Coordinator, 
explained that the company needed to protect 
its information, and there were several suppliers 
offering solutions for doing this. They had 
confidence in Movti as their business partner, so 
when Movti presented the Keepit solution as the 
ideal option for the challenges we faced, Krona 
was confident that this was the right solution. 
“POC (proof of concept) was carried out where 
our technology team tested the product. 
Through this trial, we saw Keepit’s  potential and 
how it met our needs.” 
– Gilharde Wasneski, Information Architecture and Security 
Coordinator at Krona

About Krona
Krona is a leading Brazilian company in 
the pipes and fittings market, offering 
an extensive catalog of products within 
the civil construction, infrastructure, 
agricultural and industrial sectors
Founded in 1994, the company’s main 
focus is on quality, innovation and 
sustainability in its products. With a 
successful track record, the company 
today stands out for its technical 
expertise, production capacity and 
commitment to sustainable development.
Customer case:
Krona
The process of deploying and using Keepit 
After testing and finalizing negotiations, it was time to 
deploy Keepit.
A major differentiator is its intuitive, user-friendly interface, 
allowing users to protect their important data efficiently, 
guaranteeing the security and integrity of the information.
“The deployment process was very quick; every-
thing happened within the same week. Its usability 
is truly a differentiator for both the IT team and our 
colleagues.” 
– Gilharde Wasneski, Information Architecture and  
Security Coordinator at Krona
Results and the impact of using the solution
After using the solution, today Krona sees significant 
improvements throughout the company’s information 
security process - thanks to Keepit’s resources and how  
easy it is to use. 
The solution has produced important results, such as:
•	
Greater data security and regulatory compliance 
•	
Increased operational efficiency in the IT team 
•	
Reducing the burden on local infrastructure; 
•	
Cost savings (when compared to conventional solutions) 
•	
Greater support for growth and innovation 
•	
Rapid recovery after data loss  
•	
Reduced risk of failures and interruptions
