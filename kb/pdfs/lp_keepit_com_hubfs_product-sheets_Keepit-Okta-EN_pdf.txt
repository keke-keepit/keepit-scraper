Keepit Backup and 
Recovery for Okta
Identities and access securely backed up and recoverable — 
on a sovereign platform
Although it’s known Okta is critical for identity and access management, it’s not as widely 
known, that under the shared responsibility model, you are responsible for the security and 
integrity of data stored within Okta. That’s why implementing independent backup is a core 
component in ensuring business continuity, security, compliance, and resilience.
Awarded for its ease of use, Keepit ensures that data stored in Okta is securely backed up and 
can be recovered swiftly and accurately when incidents occur. It effectively minimizes the 
impact of data loss and the burden of recovery on your teams.
Unified visibility
A unified platform for all backups 
across critical SaaS applications —

easy reporting for managers and 
leadership.
Assured data integrity
With Keepit, you’re certain that your 
backups are immutable — they can’t 
be modified or deleted. Stored in 
our vendor-independent cloud, your 
data remains accessible at all times.

whenever you need it.
