Customer case
ZFV-Genossenschaft: 
Improved resilience and 
operational efficiency

Protecting critical business data across multiple 
SaaS applications  
ZFV-Genossenschaft protects approximately 
1,100 users and about 25 TB of data across SaaS 
environments that support daily operations. 
These platforms include Confluence, Docusign, 
Microsoft Entra ID, Exchange Online, Microsoft 
Teams (including chat data), OneDrive, and 
SharePoint — systems that form the backbone of 
the organization’s collaboration, communication, 
and document management.
By protecting these applications within a single 
platform, the IT team can manage SaaS data 
protection consistently while ensuring critical 
data remains recoverable.
Customer case:
ZFV-Genossenschaft
For ZFV-Genossenschaft, reliable data protection is essential to keeping daily operations running 
smoothly. As one of Switzerland’s leading providers of food services, accommodation, and care, 
the cooperative operates more than 220 establishments and relies on SaaS platforms to support 
communication, collaboration, and business processes across its operations.
To ensure these systems remain protected and recoverable, ZFV-Genossenschaft selected 
Keepit as its backup and recovery provider — giving the IT team a centralized way to protect data 
across multiple SaaS applications while strengthening its business continuity strategy.

complexity.”

Clear pricing and simplicity 
When evaluating data protection solutions, 
the ZFV-Genossenschaft IT team focused on 
functionality, cost transparency, and long-term 
scalability.
Many competing solutions relied on complex 
licensing models or unpredictable storage costs. 
Keepit stood out by offering a clear pricing 
structure and unlimited storage, helping the team 
avoid uncertainty as their data environment grows.
Equally important was the platform’s simplicity. 
The team needed a solution that would be 
straightforward to deploy, intuitive to operate, and 
easy for the IT staff to manage overtime.  
Meeting compliance and data residency 
requirements 
Operating in Switzerland means that data 
sovereignty and regulatory compliance play 
a major role in technology decisions. For ZFV-
Genossenschaft, it was essential that data could 
remain within Switzerland and comply with local 
data protection standards.  
The organization must also meet PCI 
requirements, making security, auditability, and 
controlled access to data critical components of 
its infrastructure strategy.  
Keepit’s ability to store data in a Zurich-based 
data center gave the team the transparency 
and confidence they needed to meet these 
requirements.
“Knowing exactly where our data is stored gives 
us greater transparency and confidence from a 
compliance perspective. The ability to keep our 
data in Switzerland was a significant factor in our 
decision.”
-  Gérard Korda 
Head of IT Operations & Infrastructure
ZFV-Genossenschaft

A centralized platform for SaaS data protection 
Another factor in the decision was Keepit’s 
vendor-independent architecture. By backing up 
SaaS data in a neutral environment separate from 
the SaaS vendors themselves, the organization 
reduces the risk of vendor lock-in or service 
disruptions.
For the IT team, the value isn’t a single feature but 
the overall combination of simplicity, reliability, 
and security the platform delivers. By bringing 
multiple SaaS environments into one unified 
solution, Keepit reduces operational complexity 
and makes it easier to manage data protection 
across the IT environment.
Since deployment, backup processes that once 
required manual effort are now automated and 
centrally managed, giving the team greater 
confidence in its data protection strategy.
About ZFV-Genossenschaft
The ZFV Cooperative (ZFV) is a leading Swiss 
hospitality group with around 220 establishments 
across the country. It operates in various business 
areas, including gastronomy, hospitality, childcare, 
and real estate. The group also includes the “Sorell” 
boutique hotels and restaurants, the “KiMi” daycare 
centers, after-school programs, and lunch services, as 
well as the JUNG bakeries, in which ZFV and John Baker 
each hold a 50% stake.
Founded in 1894 by visionary women from Zurich, ZFV 
has stood for courage, responsibility, and community 
for more than 130 years. As a cooperative, it works 
in collaboration with a diverse network of partners, 
institutions, and dedicated individuals. It promotes 
sustainable and healthy nutrition, creates inspiring 
places for encounters, and is committed to equal 
opportunities, diversity, and inclusion. Together 
with its more than 2,900 employees, ZFV creates 
experiences every day that connect, bring joy, and 
generate positive social impact. In doing so, ZFV 
pursues its vision: hospitality for a society in which we 
all want to live. zfv.ch
