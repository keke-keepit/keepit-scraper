Reduce the impact and the burden of restoring your data and processes
Data corruption in Salesforce can occur due to accidental overwrites, integration errors, or 
system failures, making recovery complex and time-consuming.  Without precise restore 
capabilities, businesses lose critical data, break relationships, or restore unnecessary 
information.  Incomplete, manual, or outdated methods lead to operational disruption and 
compliance risks.  
Keepit eliminates those problems by providing you with intelligent capabilities needed to keep 
your data and metadata safe and easily recoverable, while ensuring visibility into the health of 
backups in a single platform across SaaS applications.

Granular restore - Restore specific 
records, fields, and related data.
Parent-Child relationship restore – Restore records 
while preserving parent-child relationships.
Sandbox seeding  – Populate sandboxes with 
selected data sets while preserving relationships.
Data compare – Compare backup snapshots 
to identify records added, modified, or deleted 
over time, enabling precise recovery. 
Files restore with user and object reassignment  – 
Restore files based on active users while allowing 
admins to reassign them to different objects to 
maintain proper associations and ensure usability.
Selective metadata restore – Restore 
only the necessary metadata without 
affecting the entire environment.
Point-in-time recovery – Restore data to 
an exact state from any historical point to 
reverse accidental changes or corruption.
