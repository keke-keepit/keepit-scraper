Agentic AI is moving fast.
Is disaster recovery keeping up?
Many organizations believe their DR plans cover agentic AI. 
The data says readiness is often assumed 
 not tested.
Two independent global research programs arrive at the same conclusion: 
Organizations are deploying agentic AI at pace, while their disaster recovery 
(DR), infrastructure, governance, and testing practices are lagging behind.

Both studies identify a gap between perceived readiness and proven, tested 
readiness. Confidence and capability are not the same thing 
 and the data 
suggests organizations have yet to close that distance.
