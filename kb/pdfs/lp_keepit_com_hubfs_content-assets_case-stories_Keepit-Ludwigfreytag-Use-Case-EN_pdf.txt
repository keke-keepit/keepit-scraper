Customer case
Keepit provides 
immutable backup for 
Ludwig Freytag

Compliance at the forefront 
Ludwig Freytag began its search for an audit-
proof, GDPR-compliant backup solution by 
researching European SaaS backup providers. 
After receiving a recommendation, the company 
landed on Keepit’s solution. With increasing 
regulations putting pressure on companies to 
always have access to critical data — and the 
ability to demonstrate this access — Keepit’s 
backup solution provides companies with needed 
capabilities such as GDPR-archiving to make 
compliance easier. “As a construction company 
with public clients, we are subject to strict data 
protection and retention requirements,” explains 
Stefan Gerdes, Team Leader IT Operations & 
Infrastructure at Ludwig Freytag.
Independence from cloud providers 
For Ludwig Freytag, independence from 
Microsoft was another crucial factor when 
searching for a backup partner for its increasing 
Teams and SharePoint data. Keepit’s vendor-
independent, cloud-native solution allows 
companies to store data completely separate 
from their SaaS vendor. With Keepit, customers 
can secure data in the data center region of their 
choosing, ensuring data sovereignty. “Storing 
data in European data centers was a central 
criterion to meet GDPR requirements and build 
trust with our internal data protection officers,” 
says Stefan.
“We wanted a solution that operates 
independently of the Microsoft ecosystem, 
offers easy recovery options, and meets data 
protection and compliance requirements.”
- Stefan Gerdes 
Team Leader, IT Operations & Infrastructure at Ludwig Freytag
Customer case:
Ludwig Freytag
Immutable backups stored in European data centers was a key factor to Germany-based Ludwig 
Freytag when choosing a data protection partner. The construction company’s IT department 
supports 1,600 users across more than 30 locations, using over 500 applications. Keepit was able 
to provide a reliable, independent and audit-proof backup solution for its Microsoft 365 data.

About Ludwig Freytag
Since 1891, family-owned Ludwig Freytag has 
utilized the expertise of four generations to become 
a leader in the German construction industry. The 
company specializes in planning, maintenance, 
and supervision for structural, industrial, and 
civil engineering construction of all types and 
sizes. With 1,900 qualified employees and the full 
versatility of a finely synchronized group of fourteen 
independent, individual medium-sized companies, 
optimal solutions for every task in the wide field of 
construction are guaranteed.
Encrypted and immutable backup  
Keepit’s solution keeps backed up data 
immutable — data cannot be deleted or altered in 
any way. Data is AES-256 encrypted to ensure the 
highest level of security. With strict data retention 
requirements, Ludwig Freytag could rest easy as 
Keepit offers unlimited storage and retention, 
without hidden costs. With data loss incidents on 
the rise, securing critical data to ensure business 
continuity has become another important 
selection criterion for the company.
