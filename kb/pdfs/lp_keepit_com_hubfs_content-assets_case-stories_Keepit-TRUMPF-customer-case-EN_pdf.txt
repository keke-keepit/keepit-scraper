Protecting data across a global workforce  
TRUMPF handles a wide range of data every day. 
That data is not only essential to delivering high-
quality products and services; it’s also highly 
sensitive. The company must comply with GDPR 
and other regional regulations, protect 
intellectual property, and guard against 
increasing cyberthreats.  
With thousands of employees collaborating 
across markets, the amount of data stored 
in Exchange, SharePoint, OneDrive, and 
Teams is significant. Keeping that data safe 
and recoverable is a core priority for the IT 
organization.

could safeguard sensitive design information, meet international compliance standards, and

A clear need for stronger, more reliable SaaS 
protection     
As TRUMPF expanded its use of cloud services, 
the IT team recognized the need to modernize its 
backup and recovery strategy. 
Key objectives included strengthening business 
continuity, improving recovery performance, 
and ensuring consistent protection across 
the company’s SaaS applications. Reliability, 
scalability, and ease of use were central 
requirements. So was finding a solution that 
integrated smoothly with existing systems and 
offered strong support without adding complexity.  
Why Keepit stood out   
TRUMPF discovered Keepit during a structured 
search for a modern, cloud-ready data 
protection platform. The company quickly 
recognized that Keepit’s approach aligned with 
its requirements for security, compliance, and 
operational resilience.  
European data centers were an important 
differentiator. With strict GDPR obligations and a 
global footprint, TRUMPF needed assurance that 
its data would remain in-region. Keepit’s vendor-
independent architecture also mattered, offering 
neutrality and flexibility.  
Security features played a major role in the 
decision. Encryption and data immutability are 
essential safeguards for protecting design data, 
manufacturing information, and customer details. 
The ability to ensure that backups couldn’t be 
altered or tampered with helped TRUMPF 
strengthen its overall security posture.

Straightforward, intuitive protection  
Keepit’s platform is designed to make backup 
and recovery straightforward and intuitive. With 
fast setup, simple navigation, and seamless 
integration across key applications, TRUMPF 
can focus on what matters while ensuring its 
data remains compliant and easily recoverable 
with minimal effort.
This simplicity proved valuable for the IT 
team. Features like granular restore, SSO 
and MFA integration, and an intuitive web 
interface make day-to-day management easier. 
The team can now perform precise, item-level 
recoveries quickly and give users a smoother 
experience when something needs to be 
restored. TRUMPF also benefits from Keepit’s 
right to be forgotten feature, helping support 
compliance requirements.
Strengthening compliance and business 
continuity 
Keepit’s platform prioritizes cloud data integrity 
and provides an independent, immutable backup 
environment with flexible access control and 
retention options. For TRUMPF, this helps ensure 
transparency, audit readiness, and alignment with 
regulatory standards.  
Today, recovery is faster, support workload is 
lower, and the team can respond more quickly 
to incidents without disruption to production or 
service operations. 
Advice for organizations evaluating SaaS backup 
Based on Gaiser’s experience, he recommends 
that other organizations define their requirements 
early, especially when it comes to compliance, 
data sovereignty, and recovery expectations. 
About TRUMPF
TRUMPF is a high-tech company offering 
manufacturing solutions in the fields of machine tools 
and laser technology. It drives digital connectivity in 
manufacturing through consulting, platform products 
and software. TRUMPF is one of the technology and 
market leaders in highly versatile machine tools for 
sheet metal processing and in the field of industrial 
lasers.   
In 2024/25, TRUMPF employed 18,303 people and 
generated sales of 4.3 billion euros. With about 90 
companies, the TRUMPF Group is represented in 
nearly every European country as well as in North 
America, South America and Asia. The company has 
production facilities in Germany, France, the United 
Kingdom, Italy, Austria, Switzerland, Poland, the Czech 
Republic, the United States, Mexico and China.
