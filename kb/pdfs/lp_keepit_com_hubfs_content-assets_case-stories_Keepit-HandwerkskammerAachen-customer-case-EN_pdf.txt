Customer case
Convenient and 
secure backup for 
Handwerkskammer Aachen

Stringent data protection requirements 
The Aachen Chamber of Skilled Crafts faced 
stringent data protection requirements, 
including GDPR compliance and adherence to 
federal and state data protection laws. “With 
250 users, our organization needed a solution 
that could handle sensitive personal data and 
ensure business continuity, as our tasks and 
responsibilities are regulated by federal law,” 
explains Markus Burgold, Head of IT Department 
at Handwerkskammer Aachen. 
The IT team aimed to find a convenient backup 
solution for their Microsoft 365 environment, 
with key criteria including GDPR compliance, 
user-friendliness, and reliability. Keepit provides 
independent backup of eleven business-critical 
SaaS applications, securing customer data 
in seven data center regions to ensure data 
sovereignty.
“European data centers and vendor 
independence were also crucial factors in 
choosing Keepit.”
- Markus Burgold, 
Head of IT Department at Handwerkskammer Aachen
Customer case:
Handwerkskammer Aachen
With over 17,400 member companies, the Aachen Chamber of Skilled Crafts is a driving force in 
promoting skilled trades training and innovation in its region. As a public corporation, it assumes 
a variety of tasks assigned by the state, based on the Crafts Code (Handwerksordnung) and 
its statutes. In search of reliable and compliant backup, the Chamber chose Keepit after a 
recommendation from partner IT sure.

About Handwerkskammer Aachen
Since its founding on April 24, 1900, the Aachen 
Chamber of Skilled Crafts has strengthened, 
supported, and further developed skilled crafts 
in the region. For over a century, the Chamber has 
supported, promoted, and advised skilled crafts 
businesses in the Aachen City Region and the districts 
of Düren, Heinsberg, and Euskirchen.
With over 17,400 member companies, the Aachen 
Chamber of Skilled Crafts is a strong voice and a 
driving force in promoting skilled trades training and 
innovation. 
About IT sure
IT sure, an established partner of Microsoft and Keepit,

With IT sure’s services and managed cloud services

Convenient cloud backup     
With Keepit, the Aachen Chamber of Crafts 
achieved their goal of convenient cloud backup 
for their Microsoft 365 environment. Keepit 
was able to provide a solution for backing up all 
crucial cloud data (Exchange Online, SharePoint, 
OneDrive), ensuring fast recovery in case of data 
loss, and supporting the Right to Be Forgotten 
(RTBF). The Aachen Chamber of Crafts also 
appreciated Keepit’s user-friendliness and 
reliability.  Security measures such as encryption 
and data immutability were non-negotiable for 
the organization.
Road to success through partnership 
The Aachen Chamber of Crafts became aware 
of Keepit through a recommendation in their 
network. IT sure, an established partner of 
Microsoft and Keepit, played a key role in the 
resulting cooperation. As a Keepit Elite Partner, 
IT sure focuses on modern data protection in 
the cloud, helping medium-sized companies 
maximize their competitive advantage through 
the efficient use of the Microsoft Cloud.
