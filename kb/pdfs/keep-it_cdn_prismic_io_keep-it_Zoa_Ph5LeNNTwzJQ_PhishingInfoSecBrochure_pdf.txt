How to avoid it? 
1. Pay attention to warnings from the InfoSec and other sources. 
2. Never respond to requests for private info. 
3. Stop & think before you click. 
4. Never open links and/or attachments in the suspicious emails.
5. Beware of messages that sound urgent or too good to be true (free iPhone). 
6. Do not share your corporate email address
on suspicious/malicious sites. 
7. Do not use your corporate email for personal purposes. 
8. Turn on MFA whenever you can.
