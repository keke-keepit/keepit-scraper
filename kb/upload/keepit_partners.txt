Jesper Johansen

IF-Blueprint leverages partner Keepit to help their customers protect the backbone of their Microsoft 365 data: Azure AD
IF-Blueprint is an IT service provider and Microsoft Partner offering a managed Microsoft 365 solution. As a trusted Keepit partner, IF-Blueprint guarantees that their customers are getting the best-in-class backup for their data when they use Keepit data protection.
When it comes to their customers’ Microsoft 365 data, Head of Managed Services at IF-Blueprint Stefan Hefele shares why backup for Azure AD is imperative to securing consistent access.
Azure AD: The backbone of Microsoft 365
As an identity provider, Azure AD ensures that your users have password-protected access to their Microsoft 365 data.â¯Without this access, employees may lose access to data, bringing business to a halt.â¯IF-Blueprint shares that Keepit's Azure AD backup solution ensures that their customers never lose access to their Microsoft 365 data, allowing them to keep their businesses running even when passwords become lost or hacked.
Without access to Azure AD, there’s no way to access the entire M365 environment.
Stefan Hefele
Head of Managed Services, IF-Blueprint
Easy to delete, difficult to recover
In Stefan’s experience, Azure AD data can be deleted more easily than in other Microsoft apps, making the need for backup even more pressing. While apps like Exchange and SharePoint have recycling bins, Azure AD does not — in many cases — offer this function, making it even more difficult to access your Microsoft data if Azure AD data is accidentally deleted. As a side note, while a recycling bin can function as a “quick fix” for accidentally deleting a single file in some apps, it is far from enough to protect your company’s data from a ransomware attack or larger scale data deletions.
Once Azure AD data is deleted, it has much larger implications than just deleting a file. You may not even be able to login to your account — or even your computer, making it impossible to continue working as normal.
If Azure AD becomes compromised, it has much larger implications than just deleting a file. You may not even be able to log in to your computer, making it impossible to work.
Stefan Hefele
Head of Managed Services, IF-Blueprint
Avoid recovery headaches
Without backup for Azure AD, it can take hours just to reset one PC — and days to reset all the PCs in an organization — after a cyberattack. This reset could include creating a new Azure AD environment and potentially rescripting everything. In short, it’s a lot of work. IF-Blueprint recommends Keepit for Azure AD for this reason; when your Azure AD data is backed up with Keepit, you’ll never, ever lose access to your Microsoft 365 data.
If a single machine gets corrupted, it can take up to 4 hours to reset the whole machine (without automations in place). Imagine translating this downtime to hundreds or thousands of machines across an entire organization.
Stefan Hefele
Head of Managed Services, IF-Blueprint

Backup and recovery specialist Keepit and renowned enterprise service manager Serviceware successfully deliver data protection solutions to German businesses.
Behind every successful IT company is a great product. But even the best product stands and falls with the quality of its sales and customer service.
Only those who know the needs and problems of their customers can provide them with the best advice and offer the best solutions to address their individual challenges.
In the realm of sales, the customer’s needs come first. The more aligned a company’s sales strategy is with its customers’ needs, the better the sales results are likely to be.
For this reason, large IT companies and IT service specialists must maintain a balanced product and service portfolio that includes the right solution for every customer request.
There are, however, fundamental requirements, like business continuity management, that all companies have to address, since reliable backup is a key element in this area.
As an important part of our data management concepts, Keepit fits perfectly into our managed backup service. The simple implementation, usability and reliability of the solution are unparalleled in the market.
Lars Linden,
Sales Director of Serviceware Infrastructure, Data Management, Cybersecurity and Digital Experience
How Keepit and Serviceware identified their synergies

As the market leader in enterprise service management with over 500 employees at 14 international locations, Serviceware met all the criteria for an optimal partnership.
The concentrated manpower in combination with a high degree of technical understanding made the company particularly attractive to Keepit. Additionally, the Danish backup specialist pursues a partner-first channel strategy and therefore places great value on close and trusting partnerships.
Keepit: a perfect fit for Serviceware’s portfolio
Keepit’s great strength is its outstanding backup solution. Lars Linden, Sales Director of Serviceware Infrastructure, Data Management, Cybersecurity and Digital Experience explains his decision for partnering with Keepit as follows: “As an important part of our data management concepts, Keepit fits perfectly into our managed backup service. The simple implementation, usability and reliability of the solution are unparalleled in the market.”
The fact that Keepit is an EU company with its own infrastructure (and is not using any data sub processors globally) is a further selling point. Customers can therefore be sure that all their compliance requirements are fully met and that their data remains within the territory of the EU at all times. This ensures compliance with all key European data protection requirements – both those inside and outside of “the box.”
A broad coverage of all relevant cloud SaaS applications gives Keepit’s qualities the final touch. No other provider can boast such extensive compatibility with most Microsoft workloads. Thus, the Danish backup solution fits perfectly into Serviceware’s selected product portfolio.
Business continuity management and cloud migration
Many companies only realize the enormous importance of BCM when they fall victim to a cyberattack. In the worst-case scenario, they are then left without access to their critical IT systems and structures from one moment to the next.
In such a precarious situation, having a functioning plan up your sleeve for continuing or restoring business operations is worth its weight in gold – especially since the NIS2 directive has become mandatory for many companies based in the EU.
Such an emergency plan not only helps to take the right steps promptly in this - often chaotic – moment; in fact, in some cases of hardship, it even decides on the further existence of the company.
Unfortunately, customers still underestimate the risk of losing data within a SaaS application and neglect their responsibility as owners of the data.
“Many companies are not aware that the responsibility for their data in the cloud lies with themselves. Those responsible for security rely completely on the cloud provider - a fatal error,” says Michael Heuer, VP Central Europe (DACH) at Keepit, and adds: “After a migration of company data to the cloud, you often have even less control than before.”
Therefore, the essential, expert advice of the Serviceware consultants makes the decisive difference for the joint customers - and often saves them from the existential risks of a cyberattack.
Many companies are not aware that the responsibility for their data in the cloud lies with themselves. Those responsible for security rely completely on the cloud provider - a fatal error, (...) After a migration of company data to the cloud, you often have even less control than before.
Michael Heuer,
VP Central Europe (DACH) at Keepit
The right chemistry

Personal contact is guaranteed by the regular presence of Keepit employees at Serviceware’s headquarters.
Shaping the future together
The two specialists have taken the opportunity of being a part of this trusting cooperation to look to the future together. One particular project of note involves combining the ITSM product interfaces from the Serviceware portfolio with Keepit’s backup solution. This should enable customers to hand over their data recovery requirements even more easily via the user helpdesk service portal.
Joint, industry-specific campaigns and approaches are also planned to tap into new market segments. With these words, Keepit’s Michael Heuer sums up the fruitful cooperation and looks forward to a promising future with Serviceware.
We are very satisfied with the professional and goal-oriented cooperation with Serviceware. Our joint customers benefit from the strong synergies and can continue to rely on the highest quality of service and data security in the future.
