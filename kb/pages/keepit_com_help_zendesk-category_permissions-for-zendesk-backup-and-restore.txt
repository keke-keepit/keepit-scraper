Permissions for Zendesk backup and restore
Keepit backs up whatever Zendesk data the connected account has admin-level access to. You can use a custom role scoped to just the areas you want backed up, rather than an account with full access to everything.
For each area, Keepit checks the account's access level:
- Admin — all required permissions for the area match the expected value. That area backs up completely.
- User — at least one required permission is missing or mismatched. Keepit still attempts to back up that area, but you may see a warning, and the resulting snapshot could be incomplete or empty.
- No access — none of the required permissions match. That area is excluded from backup and its toggle appears grayed out.
Ticketing (Support)
Tickets, Users, and Groups and Organizations require Admin-level access for the connector to be created. The remaining categories below (Views, Macros, Automations, and Triggers) aren't required; Keepit backs up whatever access level you grant them.
To count as Admin-level for a given area, every required permission listed in the table must match its expected value. If none of them match, that area has No access and can't be backed up.
