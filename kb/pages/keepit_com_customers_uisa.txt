Keepit and Uisa: One backup platform, total confidence
Managing backups across Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, and Salesforce simultaneously is complex enough. For Uisa, one of Brazil’s largest biorefineries, doing it without a single point of failure while staying in full compliance with Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (LGPD) was a hard requirement from day one. Today, 1,300 users and 120 TB of operational and strategic data are fully protected through a single, independent platform, ready for restoration within minutes if needed.
Three major cloud platforms, no data silosâ¯
Uisa operates at a scale where information is inseparable from business performance. Commercial data lives in Salesforce. Corporate documents and collaboration run through Microsoft 365, with a heavy reliance on SharePoint. Day-to-day operations extend across Google Workspace as well.
Compliance added another layer of urgency. Brazil’s LGPD regulation and the strict rules governing the country’s agribusiness and biofuels sector required long-term data retention and clear evidence of data residency. Both factors would become central to the vendor evaluation.
Managing backups for Microsoft, Google, and Salesforce individually would create data silos and enormous complexity. We needed a centralized, reliable solution that was fully independent of those providers.
– Rodrigo Gonçalves
Vice President of IT, Uisa
