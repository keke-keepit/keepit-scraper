Why back up monday.com?
Your data is your responsibility
Monday.com operates under a shared responsibility model. It is responsible for the security and availability of the service, but you are responsible for your data, configurations and access, and compliance.
Native capabilities are not enough
Native recovery in monday.com is limited to short retention windows and undo functions. Without a third-party backup, accidental deletions, errors, or misuse can permanently erase history, disrupt projects, and create compliance risk.
There is a high risk of a ripple effect
The cross-functional nature of monday.com makes data loss events potentially disruptive to multiple teams. Such events may lead to organization wide disruption to project deliveries, productivity and ultimately on overall business results.

Monday.com does not offer native backup options, or comprehensive recovery capabilities. As a cross-functional collaboration tool, it’s prone to human errors and accidental deletion – and this is no negligible risk. In fact, research shows that over 70% of data loss incidents are caused by human errors. Protecting and making sure monday.com data is recoverable protects your business and ensures continuity.
Keepit offers simple, per-seat pricing. Full conditions depend on the service package you choose. For more details, visit our pricing page.
Awarded for its broad SaaS coverage, impenetrable architecture, and ease-of-use, Keepit ensures that your monday.com data is backed up and swiftly recoverable at all times, minimizing the impact of data loss and the burden of recovery on your teams.
Your data will be securely stored in Keepit’s independent cloud. We run our own data centers , operate on our own hardware, and our own people manage the infrastructure.
To ensure data sovereignty, we have seven data center regions in:
- Americas: Washington D.C., USA and Toronto, Canada
- Europe: London, United Kingdom and Zurich, Switzerland
- EU: Copenhagen, Denmark and Frankfurt, Germany,
- Asia-Pacific: Sydney, Australia
Once a customer chooses a Keepit region for backup, their data never leaves that region. Not only does Keepit guarantee that backup data will always stay in that region, but no processes exist that could even transfer backup data out of that region (aside from customer-initiated restores and downloads, of course). Should systems in a region ever need to be moved out of that jurisdiction, Keepit will negotiate the move with all affected customers prior to execution.
