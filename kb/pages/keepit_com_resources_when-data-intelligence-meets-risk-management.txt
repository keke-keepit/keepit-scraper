Keepit Reel: When data intelligence meets risk management
In this Keepit Reel, Niels van Ingen, CCO at Keepit, and Trent Widtfeldt, Chief of Engineering for the East at Technologent, emphasize their focus on becoming trusted advisors that help clients realize the value of their data.
They also discuss the strong element of risk management as the value of client data is fully understood and the conversation begins to shift towards cyber resilience and the growing integration of security in all technological practices.
