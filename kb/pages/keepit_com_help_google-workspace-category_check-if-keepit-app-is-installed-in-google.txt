Check if Keepit app is installed in Google
Follow these steps to check if a Keepit app is installed in Google:
1. Sign in to https://admin.google.com with an administrator’s credentials.
2. Open the Apps page.
3. Select Google Workspace Marketplace apps.
4. Ensure that Keepit for Google Workspace is present in the list of apps and that its status is ON for everyone.
