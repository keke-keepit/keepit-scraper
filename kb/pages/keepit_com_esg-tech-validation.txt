Independent technical validation of Keepit’s data protection platform
Keepit — Simplifying comprehensive backup and security for data of SaaS applications
In this independent technical validation, ESG evaluates Keepit's SaaS data protection platform and confirms that it delivers secure, streamlined, and cost-effective backup and recovery across business-critical SaaS applications like Microsoft 365, Salesforce, and Google Workspace.
The report highlights how Keepit's cloud-native architecture, vendor-independent storage model, and proprietary technology stack combine to provide clean, fast, and verifiable recoveries — even in the face of ransomware or accidental data loss.
Key takeaways
- True cloud-native backup for SaaS: Keepit offers a consistent, intuitive backup and recovery workflow across all major SaaS platforms, without relying on public cloud infrastructure or third-party tools.
- Immutable and verifiable data: ESG validated Keepit’s use of Merkle tree cryptography to ensure the integrity of every data block — enabling clean restores and audit-proof recovery.
- Secure by design: Backup data is stored in dedicated Keepit-owned infrastructure, physically isolated from the SaaS provider’s cloud to prevent cross-domain risks and downtime.
- No tiering, always hot: All data is immediately accessible for restore, preview, or download — no rehydration needed, no archive tiers, and no delays.
- Flat, predictable pricing: Keepit’s licensing model is based on seats — not storage volume or data transfer — ensuring simplicity and eliminating unexpected fees.
- Enterprise-ready scalability: Keepit supports granular restore options, global data sovereignty, automated reporting, and role-based access controls to meet the needs of complex IT environments.
