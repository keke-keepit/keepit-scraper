Keepit Partner Update: Festive edition
Insight, enablement, and a little festive cheer
The Keepit Partner Update is a regional event designed to keep you fully aligned with Keepit — our strategy, priorities, and direction. This time, we’re finishing the year together at the Guinness Open Gate Brewery in Covent Garden, with a Christmas lunch and a tour of the brewery.
We’ll start with the knowledge you need to succeed with Keepit, hear what’s ahead, and share practical takeaways you can apply directly to your business. Then we’ll sit down to lunch, raise a glass, and see how a pint is made.
We bring together partners across roles, sizes, and markets to share real experiences and learn from one another. The focus is simple: connect, learn, and move forward — together.

What to expect
The Keepit Partner Update is part of our regional event series. Each session delivers strategic updates, practical insights, and opportunities for dialogue. This festive edition adds a Christmas lunch and a brewery tour, so we can end the year with good conversation as well as good content.
