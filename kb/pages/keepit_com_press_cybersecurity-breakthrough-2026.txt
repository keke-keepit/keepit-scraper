CyberSecurity Breakthrough Awards names Keepit âBusiness Continuity Cyber Solution of the Yearâ for the second year running
Keepit's AI Truth Cloud provides the trusted data foundation powering the next generation of enterprise AI.
COPENHAGEN, Denmark, October 9, 2026 – Keepit, the vendor-independent SaaS data protection company helping organizations strengthen cyber resilience and business continuity, today announced that it is the recipient of the “Business Continuity Cyber Solution of the Year” award in the 10th annual CyberSecurity Breakthrough Awards program. Conducted by CyberSecurity Breakthrough, an independent market intelligence organization, the annual program recognizes the most innovative companies, products, and technologies driving progress in the global information security industry.
A reliable data foundation is key as AI adoption increases. Keepit offers one-of-a-kind vendor-neutral cloud dedicated to SaaS data protection. Its unique, separate, and immutable data storage with no third-party sub-processors ensures compliance with local regulations and mitigates the impact of ransomware while guaranteeing continuous data access, business continuity, and fast and effective disaster recovery. The company’s AI Truth Cloud positions Keepit as the sovereign source of truth that enterprise AI can safely build on: data that is verifiable, governed, immutable, and proven.
“Thank you to CyberSecurity Breakthrough for ‘Business Continuity Cyber Solution of the Year.’ As AI transforms enterprise data, our immutable, verified, point-in-time backup serves as the trusted source of truth that enables customers to discover, govern, and activate their data with confidence,” says Michele Hayes, CMO at Keepit.
In this landmark 10th year, the 2026 awards program received thousands of nominations from organizations in more than 20 countries around the world, representing breakthrough technologies from disruptive startups to established global enterprises. This year’s winners embody the cutting edge of cybersecurity technology, delivering next-generation protection and resilience in today’s increasingly complex threat landscape.
“Keepit protects your business continuity by serving as a trusted source of truth as AI reshapes the enterprise. Today, data is everything, putting data resilience at the center of business continuity. Increasing amounts of business-critical data live in SaaS applications, and this cloud data must be secured in case of human error, ransomware, or service provider downtime,” said Steve Johansson, managing director, CyberSecurity Breakthrough. “Keepit delivers true SaaS data protection and is providing the trusted data foundation powering the next generation of enterprise AI.”
