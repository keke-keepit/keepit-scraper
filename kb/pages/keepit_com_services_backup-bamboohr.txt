Keepit Backup and Recovery for BambooHR
Keeping your human resources data and processes safe is caring for the people and for the business.
Why back up BambooHR?
Protecting your data is your responsibility
Understanding BambooHR’s shared responsibility model is essential for your business continuity; neglecting it leaves you at risk. While BambooHR guarantees platform security and availability, employee and company-level data and configuration resilience are your responsibility.
Your BambooHR data may be permanently lost
BambooHR doesn’t offer backups or comprehensive recovery options, therefore any data or configuration loss – accidental or malicious – disrupts business operations and exposes companies to legal and compliance risks. It’s essential to integrate it to a backup solution.
High risk of a ripple effect on your organization
BambooHR is often integrated with other applications such as Microsoft 365, SSO and identity access or payroll tools. In this context, being able to recover lost data is critical to mitigate disruptions to your employees with impact on productivity and motivation.
