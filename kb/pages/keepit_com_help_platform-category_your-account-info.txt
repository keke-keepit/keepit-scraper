Your account info
To view or edit your account settings, in the lower-left corner, select your profile icon > Account info.
In Account settings > Company details, you can edit your company name and account language.
In Account settings > Account contacts, you can change and update your primary and dedicated contact details.
Select Update when you finish editing.
