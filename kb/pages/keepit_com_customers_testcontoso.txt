As one of Northern Europe’s leading renewable energy providers, Contoso Energy operates in a highly regulated industry where business continuity and data protection are essential. The organization delivers electricity, gas, and sustainable energy solutions to businesses and households while supporting more than 4,500 employees across a modern digital workplace.
With a growing cloud-first strategy and a hybrid identity environment, Alex Morgan, Head of Digital Workplace, and the IT team required a dependable backup and recovery solution to safeguard mission-critical SaaS data while reducing administrative overhead.
Selecting a trusted backup partner
Contoso Energy conducted a comprehensive vendor evaluation, comparing several backup providers based on functionality, security, pricing, and long-term partnership potential. Beyond technical capabilities, the team wanted a vendor that offered transparency, responsive support, and a straightforward customer experience.

Built for data sovereignty
As an energy provider operating under strict regulatory requirements, Contoso Energy places significant importance on data sovereignty and GDPR compliance.
Keepit's independent cloud architecture stores backup data separately from the production environment, enabling the organization to strengthen cyber resilience while maintaining greater control over where business-critical data resides.
The availability of European data centers aligned well with the company's governance and compliance objectives.
Faster recovery with less effort
The IT team frequently supports users recovering accidentally deleted files, restoring SharePoint content, and rolling back configuration changes. Native recovery tools often required lengthy manual processes, particularly when restoring multiple items.
The ability to rapidly restore Microsoft Entra ID configurations has also helped reduce operational risk. When identity policies or security settings require rollback, IT administrators can restore previous configurations quickly, minimizing disruption for end users.
By accelerating recovery workflows, Contoso Energy has improved IT productivity while reducing the operational costs associated with manual restoration tasks.
Recovery has become dramatically simpler. What previously took hours can now be completed in just a few clicks.
- Alex Morgan
Head of Digital Workplace, Contoso Energy
