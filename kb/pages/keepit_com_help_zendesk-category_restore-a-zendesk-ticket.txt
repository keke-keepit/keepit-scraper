Restore a Zendesk ticket
Restoring a Zendesk ticket returns it to its original location in Zendesk.
During the restore process, you can add a tag to the ticket, which will be used to identify the restored ticket and prevent it from triggering customer-facing notifications or automations. Learn more
Restore a Zendesk ticket
1. In your connector, locate the ticket you want to restore.
2. Optional: If you want to restore an item from an earlier point in time, select the Snapshots Viewer, and select the desired snapshot.
3. Point to the item and select ••• > Restore.
4. Optional: Add a tag to the ticket.
5. Click Next.
6. Review the summary, optionally add a comment, and click Restore.
