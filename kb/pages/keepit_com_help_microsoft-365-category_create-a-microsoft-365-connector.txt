Create a Microsoft 365 connector
Before creating a connector, make sure your service account meets the necessary requirements.
Add a connector
1. On the Connectors page, select Add connector > Add Microsoft 365 Ñonnector.
2. Ensure that your service account is ready for the setup and click Create Connector.
3. Sign in to Microsoft with your service account.
4. Read through the permissions and click Accept.
5. Review the summary and the next steps, and then click Configure.
6. Review your backup setup.
By default, all data areas are included in the backup.
7. To start the backup, click Start backup.
Note: If you click Complete later, your the first backup will not be scheduled. When you are ready to start the backup, open the configuration and click Start backup.
