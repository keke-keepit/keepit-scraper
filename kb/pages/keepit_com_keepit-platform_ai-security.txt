============================================================

AI is the topic of the hour. Certainly, AI can bring many benefits and boost productivity, but as it evolves, so do the risks:
- Automation can amplify mistakes
- Agentic systems can change data at scale
- Threat actors magnify their reach and the effectiveness of their attacks
- Visibility and control are reduced, leading to governance issues
Keepit helps IT and Security leaders mitigate these challenges.
