Revive a connector
If you want to revive a connector and gain access to your data, you have time until your retention period is over.
1. On the Connectors page, point to your scheduled-for-deletion connector and select ••• > Revive.
2. Click Confirm to revive the connector.
3. Click OK to close the pop-up.
Your connector is now revived and you will have access to your data.
