Your user info
To view or edit your user info, in the lower-left corner, select your profile icon > User info.
User settings
In the User settings tab, you can edit your user details, including full name, email, and password. Select Update when you finish editing.
Messages
Customize your message alerts and select the types of account activity updates you want to receive. Messages will be sent to your in-app inbox and to your email. Select Update when you finish editing.
