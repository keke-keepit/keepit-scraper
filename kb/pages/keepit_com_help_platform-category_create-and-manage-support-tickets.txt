Create and manage support tickets
If you encounter any issues or questions, you can easily submit a Support ticket directly through the platform.
Submit a support ticket
1. In the left-hand menu, click the Support icon in the lower corner.
2. Click + Create new ticket.
3. Select the product area related to your issue.
4. Enter a subject.
5. Describe your issue. You can add up to 10 screenshots.
6. Click Submit.
A support agent will get back to you as soon as they can.
You will be notified via an in-app message and by email as soon as you receive a reply.
Close a support ticket
If your issue has been resolved, you can close the support ticket one of the following ways:
- Point to the ticket in the ticket list and click the check mark on the right. Then click Yes, close to confirm.
- Open the ticket, click Close ticket, and then click Yes, close.
Reopen a support ticket
If you need to reopen a ticket, you can do so one of the following ways:
- Point to the ticket in the ticket list and click the circular arrow on the right. Then click Yes, reopen to confirm.
- Open the ticket, click Reopen ticket, and then click Yes, reopen.
