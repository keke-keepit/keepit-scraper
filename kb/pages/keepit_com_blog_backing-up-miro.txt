Ensuring business continuity means including backup and recovery planning into the mix.
Why you shouldn’t rely on Miro’s native backup capabilities
Many still don’t know that SaaS applications work under a shared responsibility model. This means Miro is responsible for its infrastructure and availability, but customers are responsible for their data. All companies using Miro assume the responsibility of ensuring that regular backups are in place and that they’re controlling user access and permissions with sufficient authentication methods.
With their native backup capabilities, Miro allows backup and restoration of boards manually downloaded from the application — with significant restrictions. Larger backups cannot be directly restored through the user interface and it requires manual exporting. Miro also only backs up boards — account settings, team data, project configurations, or user metadata are not included in their backups.
Only independent backup and recovery capabilities will ensure your team always has the ability to recover and restore in situations of data loss.
