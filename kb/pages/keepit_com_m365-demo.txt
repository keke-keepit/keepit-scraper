Oops! Mistakes hurt
Mistakes happen every day. Your users and administrators have access to your most vital data, and one accidental deletion means losing the critical data you rely on the most. A reliable Microsoft 365 backup helps you recover quickly from human error.
Security threats
Data loss from security breaches, such as ransomware and malicious deletion, will lead you to lose access to your data. Without a Microsoft 365 backup in place, your SaaS data can be lost forever.
Access is key to compliance
To comply, you need uninterrupted access to your data, and any data loss or disruption, such as Microsoft service outages, may lead to failures to comply. With Keepit, it’s easy.
