Restore model-driven apps
Before you begin, ensure that the app is part of a Microsoft solution.
Restore a model-driven app
1. In your connector, open your environment's folder.
2. Navigate to Power Apps > Model-driven app.
3. Optional: If you want to restore a record from an earlier point in time, open the Snapshots Viewer and select the appropriate snapshot.
4. Point to the desired app and select ••• > Restore.
5. Click Yes to start the restore.
Important: Metadata is not restored. If the app references an entity that has been deleted, that entity will not be restored.
