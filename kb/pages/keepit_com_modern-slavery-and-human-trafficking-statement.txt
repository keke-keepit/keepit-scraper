Modern Slavery and Human Trafficking Statement
Last updated: June 2025
This Modern Slavery and Human Trafficking Statement (“Statement”) is made pursuant to section 54 of the UK Modern Slavery Act 2015 for the financial year ended 30 September 2023. It describes Keepit’s actions to ensure that slavery and human trafficking do not occur in our supply chains or any part of our business operations.
