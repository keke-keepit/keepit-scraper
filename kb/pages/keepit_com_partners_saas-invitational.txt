Kick Off with the Keepit SaaS Invitational
The Keepit SaaS Invitational is an exclusive, high-stakes competition for select Keepit partners that combines elements of fantasy football and sales. This is an opportunity for both sales and technical pros to show your best on the field.
Participants earn points by successfully advancing deals through the sales funnel, making strategic timing and deal progression essential. The first four representatives who reach 125 points will advance to the Playoffs, where even bigger prizes are at stake. The first team to accumulate 1,000 points by January 17 will win a Big Game Party. The grand prize will be awarded to the representative with the most registered revenue by March 31, 2025.
Get ready to compete, earn rewards, and elevate your performance with Keepit!
