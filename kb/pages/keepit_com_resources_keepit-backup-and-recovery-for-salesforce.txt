Keepit Backup and Recovery for Salesforce
Reduce the impact and the burden of restoring your data and processes
Data corruption in Salesforce can occur due to accidental overwrites, integration errors, or system failures, making recovery complex and time-consuming. Without precise restore capabilities, businesses lose critical data, break relationships, or restore unnecessary information. Incomplete, manual, or outdated methods lead to operational disruption and compliance risks.
Keepit eliminates those problems by providing you with intelligent capabilities needed to keep your data and metadata safe and easily recoverable, while ensuring visibility into the health of backups in a single platform across SaaS applications.
