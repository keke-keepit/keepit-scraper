Ensure resilience and compliance under DORA
Effective from January 2025, the Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA) sets new cybersecurity and operational resilience standards for financial institutions. With the DORA compliance factsheet, you’ll get a comprehensive look at the regulation’s key requirements for data protection – such as the requirement for backups to be stored logically and physically separate from the production systems they backup - and how Keepit’s backup and recovery capabilities align with them. Download the factsheet now to see what DORA requires you to do for data protection and how Keepit supports these new demands.
