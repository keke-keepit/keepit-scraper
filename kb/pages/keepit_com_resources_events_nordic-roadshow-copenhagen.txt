Keepit Live Nordic Roadshow
We’re building something new for the Nordic IT community.
A space to connect across borders, share perspectives, and learn from one another.
Keepit Live Nordic Roadshow is more than an event — it’s the beginning of a shared platform for IT peers, partners, and customers across the Nordics to exchange ideas and explore how AI, cybersecurity, and data resilience are reshaping the digital landscape.
Each city will feature its own program and local voices — all centered around one shared goal: bringing people together to inspire collaboration, innovation, and stronger digital resilience across the region.
