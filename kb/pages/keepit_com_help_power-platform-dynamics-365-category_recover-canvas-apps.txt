Recover Canvas apps
Canvas apps cannot be restored directly, but they can be downloaded as a ZIP package and imported through the Microsoft Power Apps page.
Before you begin, ensure that the app is part of a Microsoft solution.
To recover a canvas app
In the Keepit platform
1. In your connector, open your environment's folder.
2. Navigate to Power Apps > Canvas.
3. Optional: If you want to restore a record from an earlier point in time, open the Snapshots Viewer and select the appropriate snapshot.
4. Point to the desired app and select ••• > Download options > Download. 
The ZIP file will be downloaded to your browser’s default download location.
In Microsoft Power Apps
1. Open the Apps page.
2. In the top toolbar, select Import app > From package (.zip).
3, Upload the ZIP file you downloaded from Keepit.
