Enhance your IAM resilience: Keepit Backup and Recovery for Okta Â
With the growing adoption of SaaS applications, identity and access management (IAM) has become essential — tools such as Okta enable business operations by giving employees necessary rights and access to perform their daily work.
Just as with Microsoft Entra ID, cyberattacks or misconfigurations due to human error in Okta can have a significant impact on business continuity, making it a vital tool to independently back up.
Why is it important to back up your identity and access management applications?
Identity systems like Okta are increasingly targeted by cyberattacks. They're in essence the key to your organization —losing the ability to monitor, grant and revoke logins or access to workflows and applications can bring your business operations to a standstill. Even small misconfigurations, accidental deletions, or malicious modifications in Okta can cause widespread outages—locking out users or breaking integrations. These can be costly for your organization as misconfigured IAM policies can cost organizations up to $1.5 million annually — from fines, unauthorized access, and productivity loss.
Okta, just as other SaaS providers, operates under a shared responsibility model, and does not currently provide a built-in backup and restore capability for your configuration. This means customers are responsible for creating and managing their own backup strategies. With no native backup capabilities available, independent backup through a provider like Keepit is the only way to be 100% certain your data is always recoverable.
