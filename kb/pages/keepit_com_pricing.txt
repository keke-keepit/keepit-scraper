Core Continuity
Ideal for organizations with smaller IT teams , looking for a reliable, easy-to-use solution for backing up and recovering data and configurations for critical SaaS applications.
Core Continuity includes:
- Recovery for up to 5 years
- Always hot storage
- SSO and MFA
Business Resilience
Ideal for organizations with a mature, or maturing SaaS data protection foundation, looking for advanced protection, visibility and control over data.
Business Resilience includes everything in Core Continuity plus:
- Recovery for up to 10 years
- Multi-geo backup storage
- Access to add-ons to expand your data protection strategy
Enterprise Resilience
Ideal for larger organizations with a strong SaaS data protection foundation and demanding regulatory requirements, looking for top-class service and support.
Enterprise Resilience includes everything in Business Resilience plus:
- Access to a dedicated Customer Experience Manager
- Regular business reviews
- Top support priority
Explore our SaaS apps
Benefit from bundle discounts.
