The simplest way to secure your critical SaaS data
Development environments have become the operational backbone of modern software delivery. As organizations increase dependence on platforms like Jira, Confluence, and GitHub, recent platform changes, growing cloud dependency, and evolving security risks are causing teams to reevaluate how critical development data is protected and recovered.
