Can the service account be used for M365 backup have MFA enabled?
Yes, the service account used to set up a Microsoft 365 connector can have MFA enabled.
If MFA is enabled for the service account after the connector is first created, then you will need to reauthenticate your connector. In this case, you will see a message near your connector. Simply click the message, reauthenticate, and your backup will continue as usual.
