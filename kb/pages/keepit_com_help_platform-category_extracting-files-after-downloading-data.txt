Extracting files after downloading data from Keepit
If you encounter issues extracting your data from a ZIP file, the problem is likely related to the zip utility in Windows, not with Keepit. Windows has a strict limitation on path and filename length, and different ZIP utilities handle this limitation in various ways.
To resolve this, consider using a more compatible ZIP utility (we recommend 7-Zip) or try extracting your data on a different system that may not have the same restrictions.
