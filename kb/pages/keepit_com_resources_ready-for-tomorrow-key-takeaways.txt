Ready for tomorrow: Key takeaways on AI adoption and resilience
AI adoption is accelerating across every industry. But many organizations are moving faster than their governance, resilience, and recovery strategies can keep up. From shadow AI and unintended automation errors to growing vendor and data exposure risks, the challenge isn’t just adopting AI — it’s staying in control as complexity increases.
This was the focus of a recent Keepit webinar, where a panel of experts explored the risks organizations face when implementing AI capabilities, and what leaders must do to build resilience into their strategy from the start.
Here’s what we learned:
- AI at scale amplifies mistakes. 
 A single human error is limited. An AI agent can repeat that same mistake thousands of times in seconds. Organizations need stronger oversight, rollback capabilities, and observability to reduce unintended consequences.
 
- Shadow AI introduces a new category of risk 
 When organizations don’t provide approved AI tools and clear governance, employees will adopt AI tools on their own — often without safeguards around sensitive data and compliance.
 
- Resilience must evolve alongside AI adoption. 
 As AI gains access to interconnected SaaS environments and autonomous workflows, organizations need immutable backups, traceability, and trusted recovery points to stay resilient when things go wrong.

- A disciplined three-step approach to responsible AI
- The biggest risks and challenges associated with agentic AI
- Why observability, rollback, and immutable backups are essential resilience requirements when adopting AI
