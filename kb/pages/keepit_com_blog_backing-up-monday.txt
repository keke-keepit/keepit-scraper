Why do you need to back up your monday.com data?
Like other SaaS applications, monday.com operates under a shared responsibility model: It’s responsible for the security of the service — infrastructure, platform architecture, hosting environment, and core application security. Although it performs platform-level backups, it does not guarantee recovery from user-driven incidents such as accidental or malicious deletions, insider mistakes or ransomware. Customers are responsible for protecting their own data, configurations, and access management.
 
With the platform’s limited built-in restoration capabilities, users can’t do point in time restorations, and the rollback window for certain items is only 7 days. After 30 days, deleted items are no longer recoverable through the platform —making it the user's responsibility to have a third–party backup in place.
