Why connector size and total folder sizes may not match
You may notice that the size of folders within a connector doesn't match the total connector size shown on the Connectors page. This discrepancy is due to hidden folders with system metadata—data used for technical purposes.
So if you see a discrepancy in size, don't worry. All of your data is securely backed up and accessible, and this discrepancy is not a system error.
