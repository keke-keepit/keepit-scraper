Why Keepit for managed service providers (MSPs)
Empowering MSPs with Keepit's enhanced partner network
Keepit is on an amazing growth trajectory, leading the way in SaaS data protection with the only vendor-independent data protection cloud. As we drive forward on our substantial expansion strategy, we’re focusing in on our partner-only strategy, ensuring everything we’re working on is with partners in mind.
We know MSPs (managed service providers) want to elevate their services, drive success, and get the edge in the market so that they can fill critical needs of their customers in terms of data protection in SaaS applications. That’s why we at Keepit make adoption and integration of the Keepit platform extremely simple. Seamless integration for enhanced value
Our goal is simple: To provide you with the tools you need in a simple platform that provides powerful data protection and is easy to onboard and use. Onboarding Keepit is so simple that you’ll be using it in only a few clicks. That's because with our newly revamped MSP track in our channel program, we make it easier than ever for MSPs to incorporate a new tool into their toolbox.
With Keepit, you’ll:
- Get a low barrier of entry to onboard Keepit.
- Radically change your offerings and level of service to your customers.
- Leverage a single dashboard with multiple applications protected (Microsoft 365, Entra ID, Salesforce, Google Workspace, and other services).
- Increase efficiency: Easy on time and staff hours.
- Be compliant ready for NIS2, HIPAA, GDPR, and others.
- Get follow-the-sun customer and partner support.
Giving partners the edge with next-level data protection
As MSPs navigate the demands of protecting business-critical SaaS data for customers, finding the right partner makes a world of difference. Enter the Keepit Partner Network, a game changer in empowering MSPs to deliver top-tier services.
Here are some key benefits that make Keepit stand out as a top choice for MSPs:
- Easy integration: Integrating Keepit into your existing systems is easy with our API-only architecture.
- Flexibility: Work with Keepit on term contracts for one, two, or three years, or on a consumption mode and get billed monthly. The choice is yours.
- Simplicity: Our cloud-native solution's ease of use saves you valuable time and effort by simplifying the process of configuring backups and restoring data with just a few clicks.
- Retained gross profit: Not only do MSPs get to increase their gross profit, but they’re assured of retaining it.
- Dedicated cloud infrastructure: Our purpose-built, independent cloud infrastructure ensures maximum security, reliability, and compliance for your clients' data, giving you peace of mind knowing their data is protected.
- Data immutability: With Keepit, your clients' data remains immutable and tamperproof, protecting it from ransomware attacks and ensuring data integrity at all times, safeguarding against any malice, mistakes, or misconfigurations.
- Prioritized data recovery: Our granular, prioritized, and fast recovery options give swift restoration of critical data after data loss events (like ransomware), thereby minimizing downtime and ensuring business continuity.
- Industry recognition: Keepit is consistently recognized as a leader in SaaS data protection by industry analysts. Most recently, Canalys included us in their Managed BDR Leadership Matrix as a Scaler, showing our rapid growth and outstanding performance with a new program.
