Platform features
Quick and easy setup
Keepit’s platform can be set up in just five minutes, regardless of your organization’s size. Easily create connectors, add or remove admins, and configure settings through an intuitive interface — making complex data management simpler.
Instant search and granular filtering
Find exactly what you need with Keepit’s granular search and filtering. From documents to emails, locate items with ease using inclusive filters by type, date, and size. Search across the connector and restore with one click to any destination or securely share access with secure, read-only links.
Multiple restore paths, full control
Keepit’s Restore Wizard offers flexible options, including in-place, cross-user, and granular restores. Recover entire datasets or specific files, choosing original or new locations. Easily handle duplicates and restore from any snapshot for fast, reliable recovery tailored to your needs.
Comprehensive job monitoring
Get real-time insights into your backup jobs with Keepit’s job monitor. View details on past, current, and scheduled jobs, with stats on each job’s size and status. Alerts notify you of issues, ensuring continuous protection without manual intervention.
Role-based access controls
Enhance security with Keepit’s Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Assign roles for precise control over user permissions, ideal for enterprises with complex teams. Every department or subsidiary can securely manage access to their data.
Proactive data monitoring
Keepit’s Data Protection and Anomaly Detection Dashboards help maintain backup integrity and health and allow for early identification, investigation, and remediation of risks to data.
