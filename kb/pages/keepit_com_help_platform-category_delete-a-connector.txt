Delete a connector
Deleting a connector erases your entire backup - all data across all snapshots.
Connectors have a delete retention period (by default 30 days), which gives you a set time to revive the connector before your data is lost irreversibly.
Schedule the deletion of your connector
1. On the Connectors page, point to the connector and select ••• > Delete.
2. Select Yes.
In the dialog you will see how many days you have before your connector and all backed up data is deleted permanently.
3. Enter CONFIRM in the field and then click the Confirm button.
4. Click Close.
Your connector will be scheduled for deletion. You will see a message near your connector with the date when it will be deleted.
