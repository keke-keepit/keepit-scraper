Missing data areas in your Microsoft 365 connector configuration?
When purchasing Keepit, you allocate seats for specific data areas, which determines what will be available for backup when you create a connector.
If certain data areas are unavailable in your connector configuration and you’d like to protect them, you’ll need to purchase additional seats.
To do this, submit a seat purchase request through your connector configuration. Once submitted, your request will be forwarded to the Keepit upsell team, and a representative will contact you shortly to assist with the process.
