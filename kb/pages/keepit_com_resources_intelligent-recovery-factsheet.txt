What intelligent recovery looks like with Keepit
In today’s digital world, data loss is not a question of if but when. Data loss scenarios in SaaS applications are multifold, from mistaken deletion, to misconfiguration, to an impactful ransomware attack. Recovering in each of these scenarios adds pressure on IT, development, and operations teams. And the challenge of recovering data loss is getting ever more complex with the exponential growth of data, the widespread adoption of SaaS applications, and the growing cyberthreat.
This factsheet will dive into what intelligent recovery looks like with Keepit and how we can help ensure the security of your data in the face of the unexpected.
From crisis to comeback
In this factsheet, you’ll find how our solution supports fast and intelligent recovery, regardless of the data loss scenario. We will walk through common challenges during a recovery process, namely
- Limited access to data
- Limited insight into critical data
- Verifying the integrity of recovered data
- Hitting your RTOs
- Getting data back in a usable format
 
For each of these challenges, we will describe how Keepit can solve these problems in a more intelligent way. Explore our recovery factsheet now to learn how Keepit’s purpose-built, independent SaaS data protection platform can support  your company’s  recovery journey.
