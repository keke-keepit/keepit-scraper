Revoke access to the Keepit app in Google
1. Sign in to https://admin.google.com with an administrator’s credentials.
2. Open the Apps page.
3. Select Google Workspace Marketplace apps.
4. Select Keepit for Google Workspace.
5. Select Revoke access.
