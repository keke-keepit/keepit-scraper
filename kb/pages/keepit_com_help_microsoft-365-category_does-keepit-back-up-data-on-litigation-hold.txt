Does Keepit back up data on Litigation Hold?
Keepit backs up mailboxes that are on Litigation Hold as long as the mailboxes are active.
However, Keepit does not back up items in the Recoverable Items folder, which is used by Litigation Hold, In-Place Hold, Deleted item retention, Single item recovery, Mailbox audit logging, or Calendar logging.
Keepit does back up items in the Archives and Deleted Items folders.
