Why back up Miro?
Your data and settings are your responsibility
Understanding Miro’s shared responsibility model is essential to mitigate data loss and its consequences; neglecting it leaves you at risk. While Miro guarantees platform security and availability, boards, spaces and team-level data and configuration resilience are your responsibility.
Your work may get permanently lost
Miro doesn’t offer automated backups or comprehensive recovery options. Deletions and changes remain recoverable for ninety days, after which items are permanently deleted. Data loss, accidental or malicious exposes your business to disruption and to legal and non-compliance risks.
The cost of losing your data
Miro is critical when it comes to creating user journeys, roadmaps, and strategic projects. Losing invaluable brainstorming or product development data can lead to costly disruptions in product planning, engineering workflows, and stakeholder alignment.
