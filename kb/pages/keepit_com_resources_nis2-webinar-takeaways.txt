Prepare for NIS2: Webinar key takeaways
The NIS2 directive, set to be enacted into local law by October 17, 2024, implements the most comprehensive cybersecurity regulation in global history. It aims to bolster Europe’s defense against cyber threats by standardizing cybersecurity measures across the EU, particularly for organizations with critical infrastructure.
To help organizations prepare for NIS2 and shed light on what is required to be compliant, Keepit organized a webinar, hosting an expert panel consisting of Soren Skibsted, Co-Chair of IBA’s Presidential Task Force on Cyber Security, Senator Dr. Andreas Rebetzky, Security and Infrastructure Advisor to CIOs, and Mikkel Oxfeldt, General Counsel at Keepit. The discussion focused on what organizations need to know about the content of the NIS2 directive, specifically Article 21, which addresses the importance of maintaining business continuity.
