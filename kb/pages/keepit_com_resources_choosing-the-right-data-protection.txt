Keepit Reel: Choosing the right data protection
In this Keepit Reel, Kim Larsen, CISO at Keepit, and Liz Carrizal, IT Data Recovery Manager at NOV, discuss key considerations when selecting a data protection strategy.
This insightful conversation dives into the shared responsibility model, the crucial importance of disaster recovery planning, and why choosing the right solution matters. From immutable backups to shareable restore links and a vendor-independent cloud, Liz shares how Keepit became the missing puzzle piece in her recovery strategy — and why it helps her sleep at night.
