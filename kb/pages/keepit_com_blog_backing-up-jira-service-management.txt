Keepit Backup and Recovery for Jira Service Management
Atlassian’s Jira, as well as Jira Service Management (JSM), has become central to IT and service operations. It manages incidents, requests, SLAs, and customer communication—often across the entire organization.
As Atlassian discontinues the support for on-premises services, customers need to start planning their move to the cloud — including how they will back up and recover their data. There’s a common assumption that data is inherently protected, but in reality, SaaS data protection works differently—and that difference matters.
Shared responsibility in Jira Service Management
JSM, like most SaaS applications, follows a shared responsibility model. Atlassian secures and operates the platform, but customers are responsible for their data—how it’s protected, retained, and recovered.
This means that while the service is available, recovery is limited. Native capabilities do not fully cover:
- Granular restore of tickets, attachments, or configurations
- Long-term, business-aligned retention
- Protection from accidental or malicious deletion
- Full rollback to a previously good state
In short, availability is their responsibility, but recovering and restoring data is up to you.
Why Jira Service Management data needs independent backup
Losing data in Jira Service Management can directly impact service delivery. At an operational level, data loss can lead to disrupted service desks and stalled tickets or slower incident response due to missing context.
At a broader level, JSM acts as a system of record. It supports audits, compliance, and post-incident analysis. When data is missing or incomplete, organizations risk losing traceability, audit readiness, and operational insight.
Atlassian offers some native backup and recovery services as an add-on, but with limited retention and no point-in-time restoration capabilities. It’s also important to remember that having your backups stored within your production environment means you’re vulnerable to platform failure.
