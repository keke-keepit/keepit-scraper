Can the service account used for Entra ID backup have MFA enabled?
Yes, the service account used to set up an Entra ID connector can have MFA enabled.
If MFA is enabled for the service account after the connector is first created, then you may need to reauthenticate your connector. In this case, you will see a message near your connector. Simply click the message, reauthenticate, and your backup will continue as usual.
