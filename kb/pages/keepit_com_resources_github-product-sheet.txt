Keepit Backup and Recovery for GitHub
Your GitHub data. Always safe and recoverable.
Data loss in GitHub — whether malicious or accidental — can erase critical source code, compromise security and compliance evidence, and halt development. With limited native recovery capabilities, GitHub requires a reliable third-party backup and recovery solution to avoid costly delays, rework, and risks to product integrity and business continuity.
Awarded for its broad SaaS coverage, impenetrable architecture, and ease-of-use, Keepit ensures that your GitHub data is backed up and swiftly recoverable at all times, minimizing the impact of data loss and the burden of recovery on your teams.
