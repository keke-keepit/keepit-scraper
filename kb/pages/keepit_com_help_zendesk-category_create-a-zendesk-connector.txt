Create a Zendesk connector
Follow these steps to create a Zendesk connector and set up your backup configuration.
1. On the Connectors page, select Add connector > Add Zendesk Ñonnector.
2. Enter your company domain and then select Sign in.
3. Sign in to Zendesk with an account that has admin-level access to the areas you want backed up. For details, see Permissions for Zendesk backup and restore.
4. Review the permissions and select Allow.
Note: Keepit only requests permissions the first time you create a connector and retains them for subsequent connectors.
5. You will be redirected back to Keepit where you can configure your connector.
Here you can:
- Change the name of the connector
- Configure your backup
- Manage user access to the connector by selecting the lock icon
- Limit the retention period by selecting the calendar icon
6. Select Create.
Keepit creates the connector and schedules the first backup.
