Why back up Salesforce?
Shared responsibility
Salesforce isn’t responsible for your lost data. They say "keep a regular backup of your data and to do a manual point-in-time backup before you proceed with any major data project.”
Accidental deletions
70% of data loss is from human error. Users and administrators can delete or modify large amounts of data. Many of these changes are not reversible and data can be lost without a backup.
Native tools fall short
Salesforce’s native tools aren’t designed for seamless and comprehensive backup of your data and metadata. They either serve a single task or lack the flexibility and automation needed for efficient backup and recovery – making data protection manual and time consuming.

How Keepit helps you
Most alternative cloud backup solutions store your backed-up data on the same public cloud infrastructure that hosts your primary data, which potentially exposes your company to several risks.
With Keepit, your company’s backup data is stored on an independent cloud infrastructure in two mirrored data centers in the data center region of your choice, ensuring data availability and sovereignty.
Building our own independent cloud allows Keepit to manage supply chain risks and provides tremendous control over costs, which enables us to offer unlimited hot storage and predictable “all-in” pricing.
Data recovery is made easy and fast with Keepit. Choose a specific point in time and roll back all data objects, metadata, files, and attachments in one go.
Restore and recover capabilities:
- In-place restore of records and metadata
- Restore records to a different environment
- Restore multiple records in bulk along with their related records
- Restore only specific fields of records
- Anonymize fields with dummy data
- Download records in JSON or CSV files
- Download files and attachments in original format
- Securely share records, files and attachments with public links
Keep your production data safe with the ability to backup and restore data from your production environment to your sandbox.
