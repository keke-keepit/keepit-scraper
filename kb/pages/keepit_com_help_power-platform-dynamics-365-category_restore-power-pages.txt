Restore Power Pages
Power Platform pages (sites) are stored within the Sites folder, located inside your Dynamics 365 Data folder.
Additionally, there is a dedicated Power Pages folder within the environment folder, which links directly to the Sites folder.
Restore a Power Page
1. Open your Power Platform / Dynamics 365 connector.
2. Navigate to Power Pages.
3. Click on Power Pages to access the folder containing your Power Platform sites.
4. Point to the site and click the restore icon.
5. Click Yes to confirm restore.
Power Page sites are restored along with their related items (e.g., images, video URLs).
What happens when restoring deleted sites
Deleted sites are always restored as Inactive sites. You will need to manually activate each site after restoration.
Refer to Microsoft documentation for details: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-pages/admin/reactivate-website
