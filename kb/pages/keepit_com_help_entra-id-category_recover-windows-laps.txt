============================================================

Recover Windows LAPS
Master Admins and Backup Admins can preview and copy Windows Local Administrator Password Solution (Windows LAPS) passwords to the clipboard.
Windows LAPS cannot be restored back to Entra ID, downloaded, or shared as a public link.
