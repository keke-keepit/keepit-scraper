============================================================

A simple, yet powerful service to help you take control and safeguard your Entra ID (formerly Azure AD) infrastructure from accidental deletions, ransomware, and other potentially devastating outages.
What is covered?
- Users, Groups, Admin Units Roles
- Enterprise apps, App registrations
- Conditional Access policies
- Intune Device Compliance and Device Configuration policies
- BitLocker recovery keys
- Activity logs
