Keepit: The foundation AI can't build itself — a single point of truth
Date and Time: October 20 at 2:20 p.m. (EDT) Room: Future Directions Stage Session code: FD10
AI doesn't create truth. It amplifies whatever it's given. As organizations expand AI across their SaaS applications, one lesson keeps resurfacing: you can't build a recovery strategy on the systems you need to recover from. This session explores why an independent, verified, and always-recoverable single point of truth is the foundation responsible AI adoption depends on — and why trust, recoverability, and AI strategy belong in the same conversation.
In this session, you’ll learn:
A clearer understanding of why recovery responsibility has always sat with the organization — not the platform
Practical insight into the SaaS data protection gaps that AI adoption makes more urgent, not less
A framework for building a single point of truth that is independent, verified, and always recoverable
