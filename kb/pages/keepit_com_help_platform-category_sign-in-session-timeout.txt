Sign-in session timeout
For your security, you will be automatically signed out after 15 minutes of inactivity. To keep your session active, interact with the page by moving your mouse, clicking a link, or refreshing it at least once every 15 minutes.
If your session times out, you'll need to re-enter your password to sign back in. Once signed in, you can continue right where you left off.
