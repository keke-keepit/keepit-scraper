White paper
Principles of true SaaS data protection
AI adoption exposes resilience gaps most organizations don't see
SaaS has become business-critical. But for most organizations, data protection hasn't kept up — and that gap is now a real obstacle to AI adoption and innovation. This white paper explains why the gap exists, what it costs you, and how to close it.
Closing this gap means changing how SaaS data is backed up. SaaS data is structurally unique and lives outside your infrastructure and outside your direct control. Applied consistently, the right principles change your backup data from insurance for worst-case scenarios to the trusted source of truth you rely on daily.
Download the white paper to learn about:
- Why the rush toward AI is revealing how unprepared many organizations still are for SaaS data loss 
 
- Why SaaS data has fundamentally different protection requirements than the infrastructure traditional backup was built for 
 
- What's at stake when the gap isn't closed: unreliable data quietly feeding decisions, automations, and AI agents 
 
- How backup's role has shifted from disaster recovery insurance to the foundation AI and business decisions now depend on 
 
- The four principles that close the SaaS data protection gap, from ownership to validation
