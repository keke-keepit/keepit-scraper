Keepit Reel: Foundation of recovery - Data classification
In this Keepit Reel, Niels van Ingen, CCO at Keepit, sits down with Kim Larsen, CISO at Keepit, to discuss the importance of embracing a broader strategy centered on resilience.
This insightful discussion reveals how effective data classification not only underpins recovery efforts but also serves as a cornerstone for building resilience in today’s rapidly evolving digital landscape.
