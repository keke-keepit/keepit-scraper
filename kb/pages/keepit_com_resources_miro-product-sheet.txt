Keepit Backup and Recovery for Miro
From brainstorm to backup: Keep your Miro work safe
Losing Miro boards and settings can halt projects, disrupt teamwork, erase critical IP, delay releases, and force costly recreations. As a vital application for teams to plan, design, and collaborate, it becomes essential to protect it from data loss.
Awarded for its broad SaaS coverage, impenetrable architecture, and ease-of-use, Keepit ensures that your Miro data is backed up and swiftly recoverable at all times, minimizing the impact of data loss and the burden of recovery on your teams.
